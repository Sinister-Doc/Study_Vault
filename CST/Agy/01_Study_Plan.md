# 14-Day Structured Study Plan: Karnataka GPT CST Recruitment

> **Candidate Profile:** B.E. in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE, VTU 2023).
> **Strategic Approach:** Front-load Paper 2 CS core units (engineering strength), dedicate deep focus to Paper 1 non-engineering sections (Educational Psychology, Kannada Grammar, Value Education), and finish with mock testing and revision.

---

## Week 1: Paper 2 (Computer Science Core Units)

- [ ] **Day 1: Fundamentals of Computers & Hardware Architecture**
  - Read: [[Von_Neumann_and_Hardware_Architecture]]
  - Watch: Statecraft IAS Academy [Unit 01 Fundamentals of Computer (41m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OvboDQ1Fi44)
  - Tasks: System bus architectures, Register transfer logic (PC, IR, MAR, MBR, AC), Cache mapping techniques.
- [ ] **Day 2: Discrete Structures (Logic, Sets, and Graphs)**
  - Read: [[Propositional_Logic_and_Truth_Tables]] and [[Graph_Theory_and_Trees]]
  - Watch: Neso Academy [Logical Operators & Truth Tables (6m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6kYngPvoGxU)
  - Tasks: Conditional equivalences ($p \to q \equiv \neg p \lor q$), Contrapositive, Handshaking Lemma, Euler vs Hamiltonian paths.
- [ ] **Day 3: Computer System Architecture & Digital Logic**
  - Read: [[Digital_Logic_and_Combinational_Circuits]]
  - Watch: Sadhana Academy (Dr. Gururaj Bulbule) [Logic Gates & Boolean Algebra (25m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kodEefWzx5s)
  - Tasks: Universal gates (NAND/NOR minimum counts), K-maps, Adders, Multiplexers, Master-Slave JK Flip-Flops.
- [ ] **Day 4: Programming Languages (C, C++, OOP) & 2D Graphics**
  - Read: [[C_and_CPP_Core_Concepts]]
  - Watch: freeCodeCamp [Pointers in C / C++ Full Course (3h 47m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zuegQmMdy8M)
  - Tasks: Pointer arithmetic, dynamic allocation (`malloc` vs `calloc`), OOP pillars, virtual functions, 2D affine transformation matrices.
- [ ] **Day 5: Database Management Systems (DBMS) & Normalization**
  - Read: [[Relational_Model_and_Normalization]]
  - Watch: Gate Smashers [Introduction to Normalization (10m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5GDTIUVlHB8)
  - Tasks: Super Key vs Candidate Key, Attribute closure $X^+$, 1NF to BCNF rules, ACID transaction properties.
- [ ] **Day 6: Operating Systems & Memory Management**
  - Read: [[Process_Scheduling_and_Deadlocks]]
  - Watch: Gate Smashers [Shortest Job First Scheduling (11m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VCIVXPoiLpU)
  - Watch: GeeksforGeeks [Banker's Algorithm in OS (12m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hjGD08xfg9c)
  - Tasks: CPU scheduling Gantt charts, Average TAT/WT calculations, 4 Coffman conditions, Banker's safety sequence, Belady's anomaly in FIFO.
- [ ] **Day 7: Software Engineering & Data Structures Review**
  - Read: [[SDLC_Models_and_Testing_Strategies]] and [[Asymptotic_Notation_and_Core_Algorithms]]
  - Watch: Gate Smashers [Evolutionary & SDLC Models (8m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SzRs2y1TcSY)
  - Watch: Abdul Bari [Time Complexity & Asymptotics (26m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9TlHvipP5yA)
  - Tasks: Agile sprints, McCabe Cyclomatic Complexity ($V(G) = E - N + 2P$), Sorting complexity matrix, AVL rotations.

## Week 2: Paper 1 & Integrated Mock Practice

- [ ] **Day 8: Computer Networks & Artificial Intelligence**
  - Read: [[OSI_Model_and_IP_Subnetting]] and [[Search_Algorithms_and_Admissible_Heuristics]]
  - Watch: Kunal Kushwaha [OSI Model Deep Dive (30m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IPvYjXCsTg8)
  - Watch: Professor Messer [IPv4 Subnetting Calculations (12m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_bn0CF5zqvo)
  - Tasks: OSI 7 layers, TCP vs UDP, CIDR subnetting formulas ($2^H - 2$), A* search $f(n) = g(n) + h(n)$, Alpha-Beta pruning.
- [ ] **Day 9: Computer Literacy (Paper 1 Scoring Booster)**
  - Read: [[Office_Automation_and_Kannada_Software]]
  - Watch: Teacher's Academy [Computer Literacy Guide (42m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RAAi5WtSJDo)
  - Tasks: Windows shortcuts, MS Excel formulas (`SUMIF`, `VLOOKUP`), Nudi 5.0 typing, Baraha, Unicode UTF-8 range (`0C80`–`0CFF`).
- [ ] **Day 10: Educational Psychology (Heaviest Non-CS Area)**
  - Read: [[Child_Development_and_Learning_Theories]]
  - Watch: Payana Academy (Shrishail Sir) [Psychology Complete Class (2h 45m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VxWM_ZAkkHc)

- [ ] **Day 12: General Knowledge (History, Geography, Constitution)**

  - Watch: Classic Education [Cauvery River & Dams (33m)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0oPY66rWSGo)
