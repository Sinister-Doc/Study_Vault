## 1. Highest Civilian & National Honors

### Bharat Ratna Recipients (2024)
In a historic year, the President of India conferred the **Bharat Ratna** (highest civilian award) upon five eminent personalities:

| Recipient | Domain / Prominence | Key Historical Achievement |
|---|---|---|
| **Karpoori Thakur** (Posthumous) | Public Affairs (Former CM of Bihar) | Revered as *'Jannayak'*; pioneer of OBC quotas (Mungeri Lal Commission formula) and socialist equality. |
| **L. K. Advani** | Public Affairs (Former Deputy Prime Minister) | Long-serving parliamentarian, Home Minister, and architect of modern democratic coalitions. |
| **P. V. Narasimha Rao** (Posthumous) | Public Affairs (9th Prime Minister of India) | Father of Indian Economic Reforms (1991 LPG policy: Liberalization, Privatization, Globalization). |
| **Chaudhary Charan Singh** (Posthumous) | Public Affairs (5th Prime Minister of India) | Dedicated champion of Indian farmers and rural welfare; his birth anniversary (23 Dec) is observed as *Kisan Diwas*. |
| **Dr. M. S. Swaminathan** (Posthumous) | Agricultural Science | Father of India's **Green Revolution**; revolutionized wheat and rice yields, securing national food self-sufficiency. |

### 58th Jnanpith Award (for 2023, Conferred 2024)
- Jointly awarded to two literary stalwarts:
  1. **Gulzar (Sampooran Singh Kalra):** Renowned Urdu poet, lyricist, film director, Sahitya Akademi and Dadasaheb Phalke awardee.
  2. **Jagadguru Rambhadracharya:** Eminent Sanskrit polyglot, scholar, spiritual leader, and author of over 100 books.
- *Exam Context:* India's highest literary award; instituting body is Bharatiya Jnanpith (founded by Sahu Shanti Prasad Jain in 1944). Karnataka holds the second-highest tally of 8 Jnanpith awardees (Kuvempu, Da. Ra. Bendre, Shivaram Karanth, Masti Venkatesha Iyengar, V. K. Gokak, U. R. Ananthamurthy, Girish Karnad, Chandrashekhara Kambara).

### 70th National Film Awards (for 2022, Conferred 2024)
- **Best Feature Film:** *Aattam* (Malayalam, directed by Anand Ekarshi).
- **Best Actor in a Leading Role:** **Rishab Shetty** for his performance in the Kannada folklore film ***Kantara***.
- **Best Actress in a Leading Role (Shared):** 
  - Nithya Menen (*Thiruchitrambalam* - Tamil)
  - Manasi Parekh (*Kutch Express* - Gujarati)
- **Best Film Providing Wholesome Entertainment:** ***Kantara*** (Kannada).
- **Dadasaheb Phalke Award (India's highest cinema honor):** Conferred upon veteran actor **Mithun Chakraborty**.

## 3. Sports Landmarks (2024–2025)

### Paris Summer Olympics 2024 (33rd Olympiad)
- **Host City:** Paris, France (Motto: *Games Wide Open*).
- **India's Medal Tally:** **6 Medals (1 Silver, 5 Bronze)**.
  1. **Neeraj Chopra** — **Silver Medal** in Men's Javelin Throw (89.45 m; Pakistan's Arshad Nadeem won Gold with Olympic record 92.97 m).
  2. **Manu Bhaker** — **Bronze Medal** in Women's 10m Air Pistol (became the **first Indian woman shooter to win an Olympic medal**).
  3. **Manu Bhaker & Sarabjot Singh** — **Bronze Medal** in Mixed 10m Air Pistol Team event (Manu became the **first independent Indian athlete to win two medals at a single Olympic Games**).
  4. **Swapnil Kusale** — **Bronze Medal** in Men's 50m Rifle 3 Positions.
  5. **Indian Men's Hockey Team** — **Bronze Medal** (defeated Spain 2-1; historic back-to-back bronze medals under captain Harmanpreet Singh; farewell match for goalkeeper P. R. Sreejesh).
  6. **Aman Sehrawat** — **Bronze Medal** in Men's Freestyle 57kg Wrestling (youngest Indian individual Olympic medalist at 21 years).

### Paris Summer Paralympics 2024
- **India's Performance:** Historic record haul of **29 Medals (7 Gold, 9 Silver, 13 Bronze)**, finishing 18th in the global medal standings.
- **Notable Gold Medalists:**
  - **Avani Lekhara** (Women's 10m Air Rifle SH1 - first Indian woman to win two Paralympic golds).
  - **Sumit Antil** (Men's Javelin Throw F64 - defended gold with Paralympic record 70.59 m).
  - **Navdeep Singh** (Men's Javelin Throw F41).
  - **Dharambir** (Men's Club Throw F51).
  - **Pravin Kumar** (Men's High Jump T64).
  - **Harvinder Singh** (Men's Individual Recurve Archery - India's 1st archery gold).
  - **Nitesh Kumar** (Para Badminton Men's Singles SL3).

### ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2024
- **Host Nations:** United States of America (USA) and West Indies.
- **Champions:** **India** (won by 7 runs against South Africa in the final held at Kensington Oval, Bridgetown, Barbados on 29 June 2024).
- **Milestones:**
  - India's 2nd T20 World Cup title (previously won under MS Dhoni in 2007).
  - Captain: **Rohit Sharma**; Head Coach: **Rahul Dravid**.
  - Player of the Match in Final: **Virat Kohli** (76 runs).
  - Player of the Tournament: **Jasprit Bumrah** (15 wickets at economy rate 4.17).

### 45th FIDE Chess Olympiad (Budapest, Hungary, 2024)
- **Historic Double Gold for India:**
  - **Open Section Gold:** Indian Men's Team (D. Gukesh, Arjun Erigaisi, R. Praggnanandhaa, Vidit Gujrathi, Pentala Harikrishna).
  - **Women's Section Gold:** Indian Women's Team (Harika Dronavalli, R. Vaishali, Divya Deshmukh, Vantika Agrawal, Tania Sachdev).
- **Individual Board Gold:** Dommaraju Gukesh on Board 1 (immense performance rating > 3000) and Arjun Erigaisi on Board 3.
- **FIDE Candidates Tournament:** **D. Gukesh** (aged 17) won the Candidates in Toronto, Canada, becoming the **youngest ever challenger in history** for the World Chess Championship against Ding Liren.

### Tennis: Rohan Bopanna's Historic Feat
- **Australian Open 2024:** Karnataka's **Rohan Bopanna** and Australian partner Matthew Ebden won the Men's Doubles title.
- At **43 years and 329 days**, Bopanna became the **oldest first-time world number one** and the **oldest man in the Open Era to win a Grand Slam major doubles title**.
