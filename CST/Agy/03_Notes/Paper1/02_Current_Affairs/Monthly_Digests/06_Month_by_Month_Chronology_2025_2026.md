# Month-by-Month High-Yield Chronology (2025–2026)

> [!tip]
> **Rapid Revision Guide:** Use this chronological timeline to review quick 1-liner factual questions asked in KEA/KPSC general papers (dates, launch venues, host nations, appointments, and scheme anniversaries).

---

## 1. First Quarter (Q1: January – March)

- **January:**
  - *Aditya-L1 Halo Insertion:* ISRO successfully maneuvers Aditya-L1 into its halo orbit around Sun-Earth L1 point.
  - *Republic Day Celebrations:* 75th Republic Day of India; French President Emmanuel Macron was Chief Guest; Theme: *"Viksit Bharat"* and *"Bharat - Loktantra ki Matruka"*.
  - *Australian Open:* Karnataka veteran Rohan Bopanna wins Men's Doubles, becoming oldest Grand Slam champion in Open Era.
  - *Ayodhya:* Pran Pratishtha ceremony of Shri Ram Lalla at Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Ayodhya; Chief sculptor: Mysuru's Arun Yogiraj.
- **February:**
  - *Interim Union Budget:* Presented by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman; announced PM-Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (1 crore households) and Lakhpati Didi expansion to 3 crore women.
  - *Gaganyaan Astronaut Announcement:* PM Modi reveals 4 IAF astronaut-elects at Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre (VSSC), Thiruvananthapuram.
  - *Jnanpith Announcement:* 58th Jnanpith Award announced for Gulzar (Urdu) and Jagadguru Rambhadracharya (Sanskrit).
  - *Electoral Bonds Verdict:* Supreme Court strikes down Electoral Bonds Scheme as unconstitutional.
- **March:**
  - *Mission Divyastra:* DRDO successfully tests Agni-5 missile with Multiple Independently Targetable Re-entry Vehicle (MIRV) technology.
  - *Citizenship Amendment Act (CAA) Rules:* Ministry of Home Affairs notifies the Citizenship (Amendment) Rules, 2024, enabling citizenship applications for persecuted minorities from Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Afghanistan.
  - *Karnataka Water Crisis Measures:* State Government initiates regulatory monitoring on private water tankers and borewells in Bengaluru urban limits.

---

## 2. Second Quarter (Q2: April – June)

- **April:**
  - *World Heritage Day & Basava Jayanti:* Celebrations of 12th-century philosopher Basaveshwara, recognized by Karnataka government as *'Samskritika Nayaka'* (Cultural Icon).
  - *FIDE Candidates Tournament:* 17-year-old D. Gukesh wins Candidates in Toronto, Canada, becoming the youngest world title challenger in chess history.
- **May:**
  - *Lok Sabha General Elections:* Voting across seven phases for the 18th Lok Sabha election nationwide.
  - *World Health Assembly:* Agreement on pandemic treaty amendments under World Health Organization (WHO) framework.
- **June:**
  - *18th Lok Sabha Formed:* NDA government formed; Shri Narendra Modi sworn in for his third consecutive term as Prime Minister of India.
  - *ICC Men's T20 World Cup Victory:* India defeats South Africa by 7 runs in Barbados to win the T20 World Cup 2024.
  - *International Day of Yoga (21 June):* 10th anniversary observed globally under the theme *"Yoga for Self and Society"*.

---

## 3. Third Quarter (Q3: July – September)

- **July:**
  - *Enforcement of New Criminal Laws (1 July):* Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) come into legal force across India.
  - *Union Budget (Full Budget):* Presented by Nirmala Sitharaman; standard deduction raised to ₹75,000 in New Tax Regime; capex maintained at ₹11.11 lakh crore.
  - *Paris Olympics 2024:* 33rd Olympic Games commence in Paris, France.
- **August:**
  - *Olympic Medals for India:* Neeraj Chopra wins Silver (89.45 m); Manu Bhaker wins historic double Bronze in shooting; Indian Men's Hockey team wins Bronze.
  - *Supreme Court SC/ST Verdict:* 7-judge Constitution Bench validates sub-classification within SC/ST categories for quota benefits.
  - *National Space Day (23 August):* Inaugural celebration of National Space Day across India commemorating Chandrayaan-3 landing.
- **September:**
  - *Paris Paralympics:* India creates national record with 29 medals (7 Gold, 9 Silver, 13 Bronze).
  - *45th FIDE Chess Olympiad (Budapest):* India script history with double Gold in both Open and Women's team sections.
  - *Cabinet Space Approvals:* Union Cabinet approves Chandrayaan-4 lunar sample return mission and Venus Orbiter Mission (Shukrayaan).

---

## 4. Fourth Quarter (Q4: October – December)

- **October:**
  - *Nobel Prizes 2024:* AI pioneers John Hopfield & Geoffrey Hinton awarded Physics Nobel; Demis Hassabis, John Jumper & David Baker win Chemistry for AlphaFold.
  - *70th National Film Awards:* Rishab Shetty receives Best Actor for *Kantara*; Mithun Chakraborty honored with Dadasaheb Phalke Award.
  - *BRICS Summit 2024:* 16th BRICS Summit held in Kazan, Russia, under theme *"Strengthening Multilateralism for Fair Global Development and Security"*.
- **November:**
  - *Karnataka Rajyotsava (1 November):* 69th Karnataka Rajyotsava celebrated across the state with Rajyotsava Prashasti honors.
  - *COP29 UN Climate Summit:* Held in Baku, Azerbaijan; negotiations centered on the New Collective Quantified Goal (NCQG) on climate finance.
  - *Constitution Day (26 November):* 75th anniversary of the adoption of the Constitution of India (Samvidhan Divas).
- **December:**
  - *87th Akhila Bharata Kannada Sahitya Sammelana (Mandya):* Presided over by Go. Ru. Channabasappa; focus on Kannada language status, regional literature, and school education.
  - *World Chess Championship:* Historical match featuring D. Gukesh in Singapore.
  - *Winter Session of Parliament:* Deliberations on Waqf Amendment Bill and one nation one election implementation roadmaps.
