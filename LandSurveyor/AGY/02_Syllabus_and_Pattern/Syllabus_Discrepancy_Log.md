# Syllabus Discrepancy Log — KEA Land Surveyor 2026

## Overview
This log documents discrepancies between informal study aggregators, older civil engineering trade syllabi, and the official Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) 2026 notification for the post of Land Surveyor (Bhoomapaka).

---

## 1. Discrepancy Matrix

| Discrepancy ID | Area of Contention | Unofficial / Aggregator Claim | Official KEA Notification Finding | Resolution & Official Authority |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DISC-01** | **Paper-II Technical Scope** | Many generic online coaching portals list standard ITI / Diploma Civil Engineering Surveying (Chain, Compass, Levelling, Plane Table, Theodolite, Tacheometry, Curves). | The official notification specifies **Paper-II as an objective Specific Paper** comprising: **Mathematics (40%)**, **Modern Methods of Surveying (20%)**, **Computer Applications (20%)**, **Physics (10%)**, and **Geography (10%)**. | **Official Source Prevails.** Primary notes in `03_Notes/` are restructured strictly to the 5 official Paper-II subjects. | **DISC-02** | **Mathematics Weightage** | Some blogs describe Mathematics as general mental ability in Paper 1 only. | Mathematics is a dedicated **40-mark technical discipline in Paper-II**, covering Arithmetic (Set Theory, Matrices, Progressions, Statistics, Permutations) and Algebra (Factorization, Quadratics, Coordinate Geometry). | **Official Source Prevails.** Authored dedicated notes [[03_Notes/01_Mathematics_Arithmetic_and_Number_Systems]] and [[03_Notes/02_Mathematics_Algebra_and_Coordinate_Geometry]]. |

| **DISC-05** | **Negative Marking Penalty** | Few older forums cite no negative marking. | Official KEA competitive exam rules mandate a deduction of **0.25 marks (1/4th penalty)** per incorrect answer. | **Official Source Prevails.** Hardcoded -0.25 penalty into all mock tests. |
