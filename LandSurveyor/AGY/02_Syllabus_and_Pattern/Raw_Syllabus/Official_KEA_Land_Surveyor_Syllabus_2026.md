# Official KEA Land Surveyor (Bhoomapaka) Syllabus 2026 — Verbatim Specification

## I. Examination Architecture & Pattern Matrix

- `[PAT-01]` **Mode of Examination:** Offline Written Examination (OMR Based).
- `[PAT-02]` **Total Competitive Papers:** Two Papers (Paper-I & Paper-II).
- `[PAT-03]` **Total Maximum Marks:** 200 Marks (100 Marks per Paper).
- `[PAT-04]` **Total Questions:** 200 Multiple Choice Questions (100 Questions per Paper).
- `[PAT-05]` **Marking Scheme:** +1.0 Mark for each correct answer.
- `[PAT-06]` **Negative Marking:** Deduction of 0.25 Mark (1/4th penalty) for every incorrect answer.
- `[PAT-07]` **Duration:** 2 Hours (120 Minutes) per Paper.
- `[PAT-08]` **Compulsory Qualifying Test:** Kannada Language Examination (150 Marks, Minimum Qualifying Benchmark: 50 Marks; qualifying only, marks not added to competitive merit ranking).
- `[PAT-09]` **Question Paper Medium:** Bilingual (Kannada and English).

---

## II. Paper-I: General Knowledge (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಜ್ಞಾನ) — 100 Marks / 100 Questions

- `[P1-GK-1.1]` Matters of everyday observation and General Science (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ).
- `[P1-GK-1.2]` Current Events: State of Karnataka, National, and International importance (ಪ್ರಚಲಿತ ವಿದ್ಯಮಾನಗಳು).
- `[P1-GK-1.3]` History of India with specific emphasis on Karnataka History (ಭಾರತ ಮತ್ತು ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ ಇತಿಹಾಸ).
- `[P1-GK-1.4]` Geography of India and Geography of Karnataka (ಭಾರತ ಮತ್ತು ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ ಭೂಗೋಳಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ).
- `[P1-GK-1.5]` Constitution of India and Indian Polity (ಭಾರತೀಯ ಸಂವಿಧಾನ ಮತ್ತು ರಾಜಕೀಯ ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥೆ).
- `[P1-GK-1.6]` State regional issues, rural development, Panchayati Raj institutions, and socio-economic welfare policies of Karnataka (ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ ಗ್ರಾಮೀಣಾಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ, ಪಂಚಾಯತ್ ರಾಜ್ ಮತ್ತು ಕಲ್ಯಾಣ ಯೋಜನೆಗಳು).
- `[P1-GK-1.7]` General Mental Ability, Analytical Reasoning, and Numerical Comprehension (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಮಾನಸಿಕ ಸಾಮರ್ಥ್ಯ).

## III. Paper-II: Specific Paper (ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ - ಭೂಮಾಪನ) — 100 Marks / 100 Questions

### Section 1: Mathematics (ಗಣಿತ ಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ) — 40% Weightage (40 Questions / 40 Marks)

#### Part A: Arithmetic (ಅಂಕಗಣಿತ)
- `[P2-MATH-1.1]` Number System (ಸಂಖ್ಯಾ ಪದ್ಧತಿ): Natural numbers, integers, rational and irrational numbers, real numbers.
- `[P2-MATH-1.2]` Squares and Square Roots (ವರ್ಗ ಮತ್ತು ವರ್ಗಮೂಲಗಳು); Cubes and Cube Roots (ಘನ ಮತ್ತು ಘನಮೂಲಗಳು).
- `[P2-MATH-1.3]` Set Theory (ಗಣ ಸಿದ್ಧಾಂತ): Types of sets, union, intersection, difference, complement, Venn diagrams.
- `[P2-MATH-1.4]` Sequences and Series (ಶ್ರೇಢಿಗಳು): Arithmetic Progression (AP) and Geometric Progression (GP), nth term, sum of n terms.
- `[P2-MATH-1.5]` Matrices and Determinants (ಮಾತೃಕೆಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ನಿರ್ಧಾರಕಗಳು): Types of matrices, addition, multiplication, transpose, determinants, inverse of $2 \times 2$ matrices.
- `[P2-MATH-1.6]` Permutations and Combinations (ಕ್ರಮಯೋಜನೆ ಮತ್ತು ವಿಕಲ್ಪಗಳು): Fundamental principle of counting, $^nP_r$ and $^nC_r$ formulas and standard problems.
- `[P2-MATH-1.7]` Statistics (ಸಂಖ್ಯಾಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ): Measures of central tendency — Mean, Median, Mode; Measures of dispersion — Range, Mean Deviation, Standard Deviation.
- `[P2-MATH-1.8]` Highest Common Factor (HCF / ಮ.ಸಾ.ಅ.) and Least Common Multiple (LCM / ಲ.ಸಾ.ಅ.).

#### Part B: Algebra & Coordinate Geometry (ಬೀಜಗಣಿತ ಮತ್ತು ನಿರ್ದೇಶಾಂಕ ರೇಖಾಗಣಿತ)
- `[P2-MATH-2.1]` Basics of Algebra: Algebraic expressions, polynomials, identities, and factorization (ಅಪವರ್ತಿಸುವಿಕೆ).
- `[P2-MATH-2.2]` Linear Equations in one and two variables (ರೇಖಾತ್ಮಕ ಸಮೀಕರಣಗಳು).
- `[P2-MATH-2.3]` Quadratic Equations (ವರ್ಗ ಸಮೀಕರಣಗಳು): Solutions by factorization and quadratic formula ($x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}$), nature of roots.
- `[P2-MATH-2.4]` Coordinate Geometry (ನಿರ್ದೇಶಾಂಕ ರೇಖಾಗಣಿತ): Distance formula, section formula, midpoint formula, area of triangle formed by three points, slope of a line.

---

### Section 2: Modern Methods of Surveying (ಆಧುನಿಕ ಭೂಮಾಪನ ಪದ್ಧತಿಗಳು) — 20% Weightage (20 Questions / 20 Marks)

- `[P2-SURV-3.1]` Photogrammetry (ಫೋಟೋಗ್ರಾಮೆಟ್ರಿ) and Aerial Survey (ವೈಮಾನಿಕ ಸಮೀಕ್ಷೆ): Principles of terrestrial and aerial photogrammetry, scale of vertical photograph, flight planning, relief displacement, stereoscopy, and overlap (forward overlap 60%, lateral overlap 20-30%).
- `[P2-SURV-3.2]` Remote Sensing (ದೂರ ಸಂವೇದಿ): Active vs Passive remote sensing, electromagnetic spectrum (EMS) bands, spectral reflectance of vegetation, soil, water; sensor resolutions (spatial, spectral, radiometric, temporal).
- `[P2-SURV-3.3]` Global Positioning System (GPS / ಜಿ.ಪಿ.ಎಸ್): Architecture (Space, Control, User segments), trilateration principle, minimum 4 satellites for 3D positioning, GDOP, GNSS constellations (NAVSTAR, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, NavIC / IRNSS).
- `[P2-SURV-3.4]` Advanced Positioning & Kinematic Surveying: Differential GPS (DGPS), Real-Time Kinematic (RTK), Continuously Operating Reference Stations (CORS), Drone surveying in cadastral applications (SVAMITVA scheme).
- `[P2-SURV-3.5]` Geographical Information System (GIS / ಜಿ.ಐ.ಎಸ್): Spatial vs Non-spatial (attribute) data, Vector data model (points, lines, polygons), Raster data model (grid cells/pixels), map projections (Universal Transverse Mercator - UTM zones, Karnataka in Zones 43N & 44N).

---

### Section 3: Computer Applications & Land Records IT (ಗಣಕಯಂತ್ರ ಅನ್ವಯಗಳು) — 20% Weightage (20 Questions / 20 Marks)

- `[P2-COMP-4.1]` Computer Architecture & Operating Systems: Hardware components (CPU, RAM, ROM, Cache, SSD, HDD), OS basics (Windows, Linux, Android), file extensions.
- `[P2-COMP-4.2]` Microsoft Office Suite (ಎಂ.ಎಸ್. ಆಫೀಸ್):
  - MS Word: Formatting, shortcut keys, mail merge, table insertion.
  - MS Excel: Spreadsheets, basic formulas (`=SUM`, `=AVERAGE`, `=COUNT`, `=COUNTA`), absolute vs relative cell referencing (`$A$1`), charts.
  - MS PowerPoint: Slide creation, animations, slide show shortcuts (F5, Shift+F5).
- `[P2-COMP-4.3]` Basics of software solutions for land records and Information Technology:
  - Bhoomi Software (ಭೂಮಿ ತಂತ್ರಾಂಶ): Computerized Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops (RTC / Pahani Form 16), FIFO mutation workflow under Section 129 of KLR Act.
  - Mojini v3 (ಮೋಜಣಿ 3): Digital land survey workflow, 11E pre-mutation sketch, Tatkal Podi, Phodi, land alienation.
  - Dishank Mobile Application (ದಿಶಾಂಕ್ ಆಪ್): Real-time smartphone GPS locator displaying cadastral survey numbers, village boundaries, and land classification.
- `[P2-COMP-4.4]` Basics of Networking & Cybersecurity: LAN, WAN, IP addresses (IPv4 vs IPv6), email protocols (SMTP, POP3, IMAP), common threats (viruses, malware, phishing, ransomware).

---

### Section 4: Physics (ಅನ್ವಯಿಕ ಭೌತಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ) — 10% Weightage (10 Questions / 10 Marks)

- `[P2-PHYS-5.1]` Universal Law of Gravitation (ಗುರುತ್ವಾಕರ್ಷಣೆಯ ನಿಯಮ): Newton's law ($F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$), Universal gravitational constant ($G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \text{ N m}^2/\text{kg}^2$).
- `[P2-PHYS-5.2]` Gravity near the Earth's surface: Acceleration due to gravity ($g = \frac{GM}{R^2} \approx 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$).
- `[P2-PHYS-5.3]` Variation of 'g': Variation with altitude ($g' \approx g(1 - 2h/R)$), variation with depth ($g' = g(1 - d/R)$), value of 'g' at the center of the Earth ($g = 0$), variation between poles (maximum) and equator (minimum).
- `[P2-PHYS-5.4]` Mass vs Weight: Intrinsic mass (constant scalar in kg) vs weight vector ($W = mg$ in Newtons), phenomenon of weightlessness in free-fall and orbiting satellites.

---

### Section 5: Geography (ಭೂಗೋಳಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ) — 10% Weightage (10 Questions / 10 Marks)

- `[P2-GEOG-6.1]` The Earth (ಭೂಮಿ): Origin, shape (Oblate Spheroid / Geoid), Earth's axis inclination ($23.5^\circ$), rotation (day and night), revolution (seasons).
- `[P2-GEOG-6.2]` Geographic Grid: Latitudes (Equator, Tropics, Arctic/Antarctic circles) and Longitudes (Prime Meridian, International Date Line), calculation of local time and standard time zones, Indian Standard Time (IST - $82.5^\circ\text{ E}$ passing through Mirzapur, +5:30 GMT).
- `[P2-GEOG-6.3]` The Lithosphere (ಶಿಲಾಗೋಳ): Internal layers of the Earth (Crust, Mantle, Core - SIAL, SIMA, NIFE), classification of rocks (Igneous, Sedimentary, Metamorphic), physical and chemical weathering processes, causes and distribution of earthquakes (seismic waves P, S, L) and volcanoes.
- `[P2-GEOG-6.4]` Maps and Cartography (ನಕಾಶೆಗಳು): Map characteristics, types of maps (Cadastral maps, Topographical maps, Thematic maps), map scales (large scale vs small scale), representative fraction (RF), globes and cartographic symbols.
- `[P2-GEOG-6.5]` Physical Features of India (ಭಾರತದ ಪ್ರಾಕೃತಿಕ ಲಕ್ಷಣಗಳು): Geographic location and boundaries, Northern Himalayas, Northern Great Plains, Peninsular Plateau, Coastal Plains (Western & Eastern), Indian Islands (Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep), major river systems (Himalayan vs Peninsular) and natural lakes.
