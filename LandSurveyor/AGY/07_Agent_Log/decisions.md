### Decision 1: PYQ Shortage & Question Bank Strategy
- **Context:** While genuine KEA Land Surveyor PYQs exist, building multiple full 100-question simulations and 10 subject-wise versions requires a substantial volume of authentic questions without fabrication or alteration.
- **Options Considered:**
  - *(a) (Recommended)* Fill genuine Land Surveyor PYQ gaps with verbatim PYQs from closely related KEA/KPSC exams sharing identical syllabus topics (e.g., KPSC PWD/WRD Junior Engineer, Draughtsman, KEA FDA/SDA for IT/GK), strictly retaining verbatim text and explicitly labelling the true source exam, supplemented with cited expected questions.
