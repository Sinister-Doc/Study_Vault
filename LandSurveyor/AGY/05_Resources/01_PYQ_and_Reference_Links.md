## 2. Standard Reference Textbooks (Surveying Core)

1. **Surveying (Vol. 1, 2 & 3) — Dr. B.C. Punmia, Ashok K. Jain, Arun K. Jain:**
   - *Volume 1:* Chain surveying, compass, plane tabling, levelling, contouring, theodolite.
   - *Volume 2:* Tacheometry, curve setting, triangulation, hydrographic surveying.
   - *Volume 3:* Modern surveying, Total Station, EDM, Remote Sensing, GPS, Photogrammetry.
2. **Surveying and Levelling — N.N. Basak (Tata McGraw-Hill):**
   - High-yield concise handbook for diploma and competitive exams; clear numerical illustrations for Rise & Fall and Bowditch adjustments.
3. **Advanced Surveying — Chandra & Gobi (Oxford University Press):**
   - Precise coverage of satellite positioning (GPS segments), GIS raster/vector data structures, and aerial photogrammetry.
4. **Directorate General of Training (DGT / NCVT) — ITI Surveyor Trade Theory (English & Kannada):**
   - Official trade manuals matching the KEA technical syllabus point-by-point.

## 3. High-Yield Online Portals & Digital Tools

- **Dishank Mobile App (ದಿಶಾಂಕ್ ಆಪ್):** Developed by KSRSAC under Bhoomi monitoring cell. Uses device GPS coordinates to display survey number, village boundary, and RTC ownership data in real-time.
- **Mojini v3 (ಮೋಜಣಿ ತಂತ್ರಾಂಶ):** Web application for 11E sketch preparation, Tatkal Podi, land alienation, and pre-mutation boundary demarcation.
- **Survey of India Open Series Maps (OSM):** [soinakshe.uk.gov.in](https://soinakshe.uk.gov.in) — authoritative topographic sheets and GTS bench mark elevations across Karnataka.
