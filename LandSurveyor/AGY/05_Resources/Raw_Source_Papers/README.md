## 2. Provenance & Verification

- **Exam Identifier:** Competitive Examination - 2016 for Recruitment of Land Surveyors in the Department of Survey, Settlement and Land Records (SSLR), Government of Karnataka.
- **Exam Date:** 31 January 2016.
- **Original Source Repository:** Government recruitment archives digitized via official educational repositories (*shikarat.com / kpscvaani.com*).
- **Physical Authenticity:** These are scans of the actual printed question booklets distributed in examination halls across Karnataka test centres.

## 3. Syllabus Mapping: 2016 Benchmark vs. 2026 KEA Syllabus

In the 2016 Paper-II booklet:
- **Questions 1–2, 7–8, 10–11:** Pure Mathematics (Exponents, Area of shaded polygons, Arithmetic rates, Ratios & Percentages). Mapped to 2026 syllabus `[P2-MATH-1.1]` to `[P2-MATH-2.4]`.
- **Questions 3:** Geography of India (Position of Kanyakumari relative to Equator and Tropics). Mapped to `[P2-GEOG-6.5]`.
- **Questions 4–6, 9:** Applied Physics (Raman Effect radiation scattering, Nanometre unit conversion $10^{-9}$ m, Sunlight transmission time ~8 mins, Scalar vs. Vector quantities). Mapped to `[P2-PHYS-5.1]` to `[P2-PHYS-5.4]`.

For modern modules introduced in the 2026 KEA notification (such as GNSS/CORS networks, Drone/Photogrammetric aerial surveying, and Bhoomi/Mojini/Dishank land records software), questions in our mock tests and question banks that simulate these new areas are explicitly and transparently categorized as **High-Yield Expected Questions (KEA 2026 Pattern Simulation)**, while historical questions are tagged as **Verbatim Official PYQs**.
