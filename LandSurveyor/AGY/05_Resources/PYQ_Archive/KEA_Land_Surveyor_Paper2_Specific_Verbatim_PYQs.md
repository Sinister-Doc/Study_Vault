### Q1. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q1]
**Kannada:** $(-2)^4 \times (-2)^4 = ?$  
**English:** $(-2)^4 \times (-2)^4 = ?$
- (A) 0
- (B) 1
- (C) 256
- (D) -256

**Answer: (C) 256**  
*Official Derivation:* $(-2)^4 = 16$. Therefore, $16 \times 16 = 256$. Alternatively, using laws of indices: $(-2)^{4+4} = (-2)^8 = 256$.

### Q10. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q10]
**Kannada:** ರಹೀಮಾದ ಗಣಿತ ತಜ್ಞರೊಬ್ಬರು $1/h$ ಗಂಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ $q$ ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಗಳನ್ನು ಬಿಡಿಸಬಲ್ಲರು. ಈ ವೇಗದಲ್ಲಿ ಅವರು $q$ ಗಂಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಎಷ್ಟು ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆಗಳನ್ನು ಬಿಡಿಸಬಲ್ಲರು?  
**English:** A Russian mathematician can solve $q$ questions in $1/h$ hours. At this rate how many questions can she solve in $q$ hours?
- (A) $hq$
- (B) $hq^2$
- (C) $1/hq$
- (D) $h/q$

**Answer: (B) $hq^2$**  
*Official Derivation:* Rate of solving = $\frac{\text{Questions}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{q}{1/h} = hq$ questions per hour.  
In $q$ hours, total questions solved = $\text{Rate} \times \text{Time} = (hq) \times q = hq^2$.

### Q11. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q11]
**Kannada:** ಬೆಂಗಳೂರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅಂತರ್ಜಾಲ ಉಪಯೋಗಿಸುವ ಪುರುಷ ಮತ್ತು ಮಹಿಳೆಯ ಅನುಪಾತ 1:4 ಇದೆ. ಪುರುಷ ಬಳಕೆದಾರರ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ ಶೇಕಡ 20 ರಷ್ಟು ಹೆಚ್ಚಿದ್ದು, ಮಹಿಳಾ ಬಳಕೆದಾರರ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ ಶೇಕಡ 10 ರಷ್ಟು ಹೆಚ್ಚಿದೆ. ಒಟ್ಟಾರೆ ಬೆಂಗಳೂರಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಅಂತರ್ಜಾಲ ಬಳಕೆದಾರರ ಹೆಚ್ಚಳದ ಪ್ರಮಾಣ ಎಷ್ಟು?  
**English:** In Bengaluru ratio of male to female internet users is 1:4. Number of male users is increased by 20% and that of female users is increased by 10%. What is the overall increase in internet users in Bengaluru?
- (A) 30%
- (B) 12%
- (C) 10%
- (D) 60%

**Answer: (B) 12%**  
*Official Derivation:* Let male users = 100, female users = 400. Total users = 500.  
Increase in male users = $20\% \text{ of } 100 = 20$.  
Increase in female users = $10\% \text{ of } 400 = 40$.  
Total increase = $20 + 40 = 60$.  
Overall percentage increase = $\frac{60}{500} \times 100 = 12\%$.

### Q23. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q23]
**English:** A gardener brought 5 rabbits, after 2 months rabbits became 10, and after 4 months they became 20. If the growth continues on the same ratio, what would be the amount of rabbits after 1 year?
- (A) 300
- (B) 320
- (C) 160
- (D) 640

**Answer: (B) 320**  
*Official Derivation:* The population doubles every 2 months. In 1 year (12 months), there are $\frac{12}{2} = 6$ doubling periods.  
Population after 6 periods = $5 \times 2^6 = 5 \times 64 = 320$.

### Q24. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q24]
**English:** A dice is tossed only once. What is the probability that the number is less than 3?
- (A) 0
- (B) 1/4
- (C) 1/3
- (D) 1/2

**Answer: (C) 1/3**  
*Official Derivation:* Sample space $S = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$, $n(S) = 6$.  
Favourable event $E = \{1, 2\}$, $n(E) = 2$.  
$P(E) = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$.

### Q26. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q26]
**English:** The difference between squares of consecutive natural numbers forms a series that is a:
- (A) Series of Even Numbers
- (B) Series of Odd Numbers
- (C) Series of Alternating Even and Odd Numbers
- (D) None of the above

**Answer: (B) Series of Odd Numbers**  
*Official Derivation:* Let consecutive numbers be $n$ and $n+1$.  
Difference of squares = $(n+1)^2 - n^2 = (n^2 + 2n + 1) - n^2 = 2n + 1$.  
For any natural number $n \ge 1$, $2n+1$ produces odd integers: $2(1)+1=3, 2(2)+1=5, 2(3)+1=7 \dots$, forming the series of odd numbers.

### Q28. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q28]
**English:** The radius of circle is increased by 20%, its area is increased by:
- (A) 44%
- (B) 40%
- (C) 20%
- (D) 48%

**Answer: (A) 44%**  
*Official Derivation:* Area $A \propto r^2$. New radius $r' = 1.2r$.  
New area $A' = \pi (1.2r)^2 = 1.44 \pi r^2 = 1.44 A$.  
Percentage increase = $(1.44 - 1) \times 100 = 44\%$.

### Q30. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q30]
**English:** Find the 50th term of the sequence $5, -2, -9, -16, \dots$
- (A) -352
- (B) -338
- (C) -343
- (D) -331

**Answer: (B) -338**  
*Official Derivation:* First term $a = 5$. Common difference $d = -2 - 5 = -7$.  
$T_n = a + (n-1)d \implies T_{50} = 5 + (49)(-7) = 5 - 343 = -338$.

### Q32. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q32]
**English:** Find the sum $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 0 & 3 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$
- (A) $\begin{bmatrix} 5 & 7 & 6 \\ 7 & 11 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$
- (B) $\begin{bmatrix} 4 & 6 & 8 \\ 7 & 8 & 12 \end{bmatrix}$
- (C) $\begin{bmatrix} 14 & 25 & 6 \\ 07 & 38 & 9 \end{bmatrix}$
- (D) None of the above

**Answer: (D) None of the above**  
*Official Derivation:* Matrix addition is defined **only** for matrices of identical dimensions (same number of rows and columns). Here Matrix 1 is of order $2 \times 2$, while Matrix 2 is of order $2 \times 3$. Therefore, matrix addition is **undefined**.

### Q34. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q34]
**English:** From a group of 7 men and 6 women, five persons are to be selected to form a committee so that at least 3 men are there on the committee. In how many ways can it be done?
- (A) 564
- (B) 645
- (C) 735
- (D) 756

**Answer: (D) 756**  
*Official Derivation:* Cases for "at least 3 men":
1. 3 men and 2 women: $^7C_3 \times ^6C_2 = 35 \times 15 = 525$
2. 4 men and 1 woman: $^7C_4 \times ^6C_1 = 35 \times 6 = 210$
3. 5 men and 0 women: $^7C_5 \times ^6C_0 = 21 \times 1 = 21$  
Total ways = $525 + 210 + 21 = 756$.

### Q4. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q4]
**Kannada:** ಸರ್ ಸಿ. ವಿ. ರಾಮನ್‌ರವರಿಗೆ ವಿಕಿರಣದ (radiation) ವಿದ್ಯಮಾನ ಕುರಿತ ಯಾವ ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ಕೆ ನೋಬೆಲ್ ಪ್ರಶಸ್ತಿ ನೀಡಲಾಗಿದೆ?  
**English:** Sir C.V. Raman was awarded Nobel Prize for his work connected with which of the following phenomenon of radiation?
- (A) Scattering (ಚದುರುವಿಕೆ)
- (B) Diffraction (ವಿವರ್ತನೆ)
- (C) Interference (ಹಸ್ತಕ್ಷೇಪ)
- (D) Polarization (ಧ್ರುವೀಕರಣ)

**Answer: (A) Scattering**  
*Explanation:* Sir C.V. Raman was awarded the 1930 Nobel Prize in Physics for his discovery of the inelastic scattering of light by molecules, now known universally as the **Raman Effect**.

### Q6. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q6]
**Kannada:** ಸೂರ್ಯನಿಂದ ಬಂದ ಬೆಳಕು ನಮ್ಮನ್ನು ತಲುಪುವ ಸಮಯ ಸುಮಾರು:  
**English:** Light from the Sun reaches us in nearly:
- (A) 2 minutes
- (B) 4 minutes
- (C) 8 minutes
- (D) 12 minutes

**Answer: (C) 8 minutes**  
*Explanation:* Mean distance from Earth to Sun $\approx 1.496 \times 10^{11}$ m. Speed of light $c \approx 3 \times 10^8$ m/s. Time $t = \frac{1.496 \times 10^{11}}{3 \times 10^8} \approx 499 \text{ seconds} \approx 8 \text{ minutes } 19 \text{ seconds}$.

### Q9. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q9]
**Kannada:** ಸ್ಕೇಲಾರ್ ಪ್ರಮಾಣವನ್ನು ಆರಿಸಿ:  
**English:** Pick out the scalar quantity:
- (A) Force (ಬಲ)
- (B) Pressure (ಒತ್ತಡ)
- (C) Velocity (ವೇಗ)
- (D) Acceleration (ವೇಗೋತ್ಕರ್ಷ)

**Answer: (B) Pressure**  
*Explanation:* Pressure is defined as force per unit area acting normal to a surface, but at any point in a fluid, pressure acts in all directions equally. It is a **scalar quantity**. Force, velocity, and acceleration possess both magnitude and direction and are vector quantities.

- (C) electromagnetic

### Q15. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q15]
**English:** Sound produced at a point is heard by a person after 5 seconds, while the same sound is heard by another person after 6 seconds. If the speed of sound is 300 m/s, what could be the maximum and minimum distances between the two persons?
- (A) 1.8 km, 0.15 km
- (B) 2.2 km, 0.2 km
- (C) 2.8 km, 0.25 km
- (D) 3.3 km, 0.30 km

**Answer: (D) 3.3 km, 0.30 km**  
*Explanation:* Distance of person 1 from source $d_1 = 5 \times 300 = 1500\text{ m} = 1.5\text{ km}$.  
Distance of person 2 from source $d_2 = 6 \times 300 = 1800\text{ m} = 1.8\text{ km}$.  
Maximum distance (when persons are on opposite sides of source): $1.5 + 1.8 = 3.3\text{ km}$.  
Minimum distance (when persons are on same line and side of source): $1.8 - 1.5 = 0.30\text{ km}$ ($300\text{ m}$).

### Q18. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q18]
**English:** If the resultant of two equal forces has the same magnitude as either of the forces, then angle between the two forces is:
- (A) $30^\circ$
- (B) $60^\circ$
- (C) $90^\circ$
- (D) $120^\circ$

**Answer: (D) $120^\circ$**  
*Explanation:* Resultant $R = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2 + 2PQ\cos\theta}$. Since $P = Q = R$:  
$P^2 = P^2 + P^2 + 2P^2\cos\theta \implies P^2 = 2P^2(1 + \cos\theta) \implies \frac{1}{2} = 1 + \cos\theta \implies \cos\theta = -\frac{1}{2} \implies \theta = 120^\circ$.

### Q20. [Official Land Surveyor 2016, Paper-II, Q20]
**English:** Minimum number of unequal vectors which can give zero resultant are:
- (A) two
- (B) three
- (C) four
- (D) more than four

**Answer: (B) three**  
*Explanation:* Two equal and opposite vectors can yield zero resultant. However, for **unequal** coplanar vectors, a minimum of **three** vectors forming a closed triangle ($\vec{A} + \vec{B} + \vec{C} = 0$) is required to produce a zero resultant.
