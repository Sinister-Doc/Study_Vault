## Section 1: Karnataka History & Culture (`P1-GK-1.3`)

### Q1. [KEA Land Surveyor]
The famous Halmidi inscription, regarded as the earliest recorded Kannada stone inscription, belongs to the period of which dynasty?
- (A) Gangas of Talakad
- (B) Kadambas of Banavasi
- (C) Chalukyas of Badami
- (D) Rashtrakutas
**Answer: (B) Kadambas of Banavasi**  
*Explanation:* The Halmidi inscription dates back to approximately 450 AD during the reign of King Kakusthavarma of the Kadamba dynasty.

### Q2. [KEA Land Surveyor]
Who was the military commander and trusted lieutenant of Kittur Rani Chennamma who continued the guerrilla warfare against the British East India Company until his execution at Nandagad in 1831?
- (A) Babasaheb of Naralgund
- (B) Sangolli Rayanna
- (C) Sindhura Lakshmana
- (D) Mundargi Bima Rao
**Answer: (B) Sangolli Rayanna**  
*Explanation:* Sangolli Rayanna was the brave army chief of Kittur who organized peasant resistance before being captured and hanged by the British at Nandagad in Belagavi district on 26 January 1831.

### Q3. [KEA Land Surveyor]
The world-renowned Aihole inscription, composed by the court poet Ravikirti describing the military victories of Pulakeshin II over Emperor Harshavardhana on the banks of Narmada, is written in which script?
- (A) Brahmi
- (B) Old Kannada (Hale Kannada)
- (C) Sanskrit in Southern Brahmi / Old Kannada script
- (D) Nagari
**Answer: (C) Sanskrit in Southern Brahmi / Old Kannada script**  
*Explanation:* The Aihole inscription (dated 634 AD) was composed in classical Sanskrit verse by Ravikirti using the Southern variety of ancient Kannada-Telugu script.

## Section 2: Karnataka & India Geography (`P1-GK-1.4`)

### Q4. [KEA Land Surveyor]
Which is the highest peak in the Western Ghats located within the state of Karnataka?
- (A) Kudremukh
- (B) Mullayanagiri
- (C) Pushpagiri
- (D) Brahmagiri
**Answer: (B) Mullayanagiri**  
*Explanation:* Mullayanagiri in the Baba Budan Giri range of Chikkamagaluru district stands at 1,930 meters (6,330 ft) and is Karnataka's highest peak.

### Q5. [KEA Land Surveyor]
The river Kaveri originates at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri hills of which district of Karnataka?
- (A) Hassan
- (B) Kodagu
- (C) Chikkamagaluru
- (D) Shivamogga
**Answer: (B) Kodagu**  
*Explanation:* Kaveri originates at Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri range of Kodagu district at an altitude of 1,341 meters.

## Section 3: Indian Constitution & Polity (`P1-GK-1.5`)

### Q6. [KEA Land Surveyor]
Which Article of the Indian Constitution provides special constitutional provisions and developmental reservation for the Kalyana-Karnataka (Hyderabad-Karnataka) region?
- (A) Article 371A
- (B) Article 371D
- (C) Article 371J
- (D) Article 371F
**Answer: (C) Article 371J**  
*Explanation:* Article 371J was inserted by the 98th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2012, providing special provisions and reservation in education and state government employment for the six backward districts of Kalyana-Karnataka.

### Q7. [KEA Land Surveyor]
The 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992, added which Schedule to the Constitution of India, listing 29 functional matters for Panchayati Raj Institutions?
- (A) Ninth Schedule
- (B) Tenth Schedule
- (C) Eleventh Schedule
- (D) Twelfth Schedule
**Answer: (C) Eleventh Schedule**  
*Explanation:* The 73rd Amendment added Part IX and the Eleventh Schedule containing 29 subjects to empower rural local self-governments.

## Section 4: General Science & Mental Ability (`P1-GK-1.1`, `P1-GK-1.7`)

### Q8. [KEA Land Surveyor]
An electric bulb of rating 100 W is operated for 10 hours daily. How many units (kWh) of electrical energy will it consume in a month of 30 days?
- (A) 10 units
- (B) 30 units
- (C) 100 units
- (D) 300 units
**Answer: (B) 30 units**  
*Explanation:* Energy consumed per day $= \frac{100\text{ W} \times 10\text{ h}}{1000} = 1\text{ kWh (unit)}$. In 30 days, total consumption $= 1 \times 30 = 30\text{ units}$.

### Q9. [KEA Land Surveyor]
Find the missing number in the sequence: $3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?$
- (A) 44
- (B) 46
- (C) 48
- (D) 50
**Answer: (C) 48**  
*Explanation:* The pattern represents $n^2 - 1$: $2^2-1=3, 3^2-1=8, 4^2-1=15, 5^2-1=24, 6^2-1=35, 7^2-1=48$.
