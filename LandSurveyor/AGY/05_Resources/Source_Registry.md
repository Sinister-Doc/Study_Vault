## 2. Statutory Acts & Government Regulations

1. **Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 (Act No. 12 of 1964):**
   - *Section 128:* Acquisition of rights to be reported.
   - *Section 129:* Preparation and maintenance of record of rights; mutation register; procedure for disposal of objections (FIFO).
   - *Section 139 & 140:* Settlement of boundary disputes and boundary marks maintenance.
2. **Karnataka Land Revenue Rules, 1966:**
   - *Form 16:* Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops (RTC / Pahani).
   - *Rules governing Phodi and Tatkal Podi.*
3. **Karnataka Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Raj Act, 1993 (Amended 2015/2016):**
   - Three-tier Panchayati Raj institutions, 11th Schedule 29 subjects, e-Swathu property certificates.

## 5. Question Classification & Provenance Standard

To maintain absolute academic honesty and rigor:
- **Verbatim Official PYQs:** Questions transcribed directly from the official 2016 test booklet (`Karnataka_Land_Surveyor_Paper_2_Official_2016.pdf`). They carry exact question numbers and mathematical derivations matching the official key.
- **High-Yield Expected Questions (Model Simulations):** Created to provide comprehensive exam-hall simulation for syllabus topics newly introduced in the 2026 notification (e.g., Bhoomi 3.0 / Mojini / Dishank IT workflows, GNSS/CORS network operations, and modern 2026 state schemes). These are strictly labeled as expected/model questions to prevent any false attribution.
