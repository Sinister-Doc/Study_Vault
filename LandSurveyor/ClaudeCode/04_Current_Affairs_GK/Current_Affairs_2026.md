## 1. Karnataka — Land & Revenue (highest priority for a surveyor)

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Bhoomi portal (ಭೂಮಿ) | Karnataka's online land-records service (RTC/Pahani, mutation, survey extracts), run by the Revenue Dept's Bhoomi Monitoring Cell. *(Exact current version number is volatile — see parking lot.)* |
| DSLR / SSLR | **Survey, Settlement & Land Records** department (ಸರ್ವೆ, ಸೆಟೆಲ್‌ಮೆಂಟ್ ಮತ್ತು ಭೂ ದಾಖಲೆ ಇಲಾಖೆ) under Revenue Dept — **this exam recruits Surveyors (Bhoomapaka) for it.** |
| DILRMP | Central **Digital India Land Records Modernisation Programme** — integrates RoR + maps + registration; Karnataka participates. |
| SVAMITVA (ಸ್ವಾಮಿತ್ವ) | Central scheme using **drones** to map inhabited (abadi) rural land and issue property cards. Directly relevant to the modern-survey Paper 2. *(Karnataka rollout numbers: parking lot.)* |
| RTC / Khata | RTC = **Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops (ಪಹಣಿ)**; Khata = assessment account. Details in [[03_Notes/05_Karnataka_Land_Revenue]]. |

## 4. Economy

- **India is the ~6th largest economy** by nominal GDP per the IMF April 2026 WEO (~US$4.15 tn for 2026) — the older "5th largest" line is out of date (rupee depreciation + GDP base-year revision pushed India behind Japan and the UK). *(Khaleej Times / Daily Pioneer citing IMF WEO Apr 2026.)*
- **GST 2.0 is implemented, not "under discussion":** the 56th GST Council (3 Sep 2025) moved to **two main slabs, 5% and 18%, plus a 40% demerit rate**, effective **22 Sep 2025**.
- Know **India's G20 presidency (2023)** and the **Viksit Bharat @2047** vision (developed-nation goal by 2047).
