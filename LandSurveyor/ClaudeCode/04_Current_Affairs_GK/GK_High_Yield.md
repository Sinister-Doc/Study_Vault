### Karnataka Geography — surveyor-exam gold

| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Regions | Malnad (hills), Maidan (plateau), Karavali (coast ~320 km) |
| Highest peak | **Mullayanagiri, 1,930 m** (Baba Budangiri, Chikkamagaluru) |
| Rivers | **Kaveri (ಗಂಗ)** — originates Talakaveri, Brahmagiri, Kodagu; "Ganga of the South". **Krishna** — originates Mahabaleshwar (outside KA) but flows across north KA; Tungabhadra (Hyderabad dam/Kurnool), Malaprabha, Ghataprabha, **Sharavathi (west-flowing → Jog Falls)**, Netravati |
| Waterfalls | **Jog Falls ~153 m** — highest plunge waterfall in India (Shimoga/Shivamogga); **Shivanasamudra** — split falls on Kaveri, **Asia's first hydroelectric station (1902)** |
| Soils | **Black cotton soil (ರೆಗು)** in north KA (Deccan traps, basalt — good for the exam: regur = self-mulching, retains moisture); **laterite** in coastal/Kodagu hills |
| Dams | Krishna Raja Sagara (KRS, M. Visvesvaraya era), Almatti, Tungabhadra, Linganamakki |
| Famous sites | **Hampi** (Vijayanagara, UNESCO), **Pattadakal** (UNESCO), **Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas** — Belur, Halebid, Somnathapura (UNESCO 2023), Badami caves, **Shravanabelagola Gommateshwara** — 57-ft monolith, tallest free-standing in the world |
| History hooks | Chalukyas of Badami (Pulakeshin II) → Rashtrakutas (Amoghavarsha) → Western Chalukyas of Kalyana → Hoysalas (Ballala II; Chennakeshava temple Belur) → Vijayanagara (Krishnadevaraya) → Wodeyars of Mysore + Tipu Sultan. Modern: **first CM of Mysore State = Kengal Hanumanthaiah (1956)**; **first BJP CM = B.S. Yediyurappa (2008)** |

**Mnemonic — rivers**: east-flowing "**KK-T**" (Kaveri, Krishna, Tungabhadra) → Bay of Bengal; west-flowing "**SN**" (Sharavathi, Netravati) → Arabian Sea.

## 9. Indian Polity Quick Facts (ರಾಜ್ಯಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ)

- Constitution: originally **395 Articles, 22 Parts, 8 Schedules**; now **25 Parts, 12 Schedules** — lengthiest written constitution; drafted by **Drafting Committee — Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (chair)**, adopted **26 Nov 1949**, enforced **26 Jan 1950**.
- **Fundamental Rights** = Part III (Art 12–35); six FRs (Right to Equality 14, Freedom 19, Constitutional Remedies **Art 32** = "heart and soul" — Ambedkar).
- **DPSP** = Part IV (Art 36–51), non-justiciable; **Fundamental Duties** = Art 51A via **42nd Amendment 1976** (Swaran Singh Committee).
- Amendment power **Art 368**; emergency provisions Part XVIII; President Art 52 (term 5 yrs), Governor Art 153 (appointed by President, doctrine/role per Sarkaria & Bommai — **S.R. Bommai 1994 case is Karnataka-linked and exam-favourite**).
- Panchayati Raj 3-tier (village–block/different in KA: gram panchayat → **taluk panchayat** → zilla panchayat) via **73rd Amendment 1992**; urban = **74th Amendment**.
- Parliament: Lok Sabha (max strength 552; current **543** elected seats) + Rajya Sabha (250 max, 245 current; 12 presidential nominations).
