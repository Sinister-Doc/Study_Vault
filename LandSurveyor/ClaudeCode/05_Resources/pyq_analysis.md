### A. Surveying (23 Q) — expected share of the "Surveying & Land Measurement" weightage

**Q1.** The length of Gunter's chain is:
A) 20 m B) 66 ft C) 100 ft D) 30 m
**Answer: B.** Basis: core definition repeatedly tested in survey exams — Gunter's chain = 66 ft with 100 links.

**Q2.** A metric chain is normally:
A) 10 m B) 20 m C) 30 m D) 50 m
**Answer: B.** Basis: standard instrument definition; metric chain = 20 m, 100 links of 20 cm each.

**Q3.** The principle of chain surveying is:
A) Trilateration B) Triangulation C) Traversing D) Resection
**Answer: B.** Basis: foundational principle — chain surveying works by dividing the area into well-conditioned triangles.

**Q4.** A well-conditioned triangle has no angle less than:
A) 45° B) 60° C) 30° D) 90°
**Answer: C.** Basis: standard conditioning rule — angles should lie between 30° and 120° for accurate intersection.

**Q5.** Offsets in chain surveying are measured:
A) Along the chain line B) Perpendicular to the chain line C) At 45° D) Any convenient angle
**Answer: B.** Basis: definition of offsets; perpendicular offsets (ordinates) are the normal method.

**Q6.** The instrument used to set out a right angle on a chain line is:
A) Ranging rod B) Arrows C) Optical square D) Plumb bob
**Answer: C.** Basis: standard instrument-question; cross staff/optical square set 90° offsets.

**Q7.** The main line that should be the longest and run through level ground is the:
A) Tie line B) Base/boundary line C) Offset line D) Check line
**Answer: B.** Basis: base line selection is a classic survey-exam topic.

**Q8.** A tie line in chain surveying is chiefly used to:
A) Check accuracy of the framework and locate interior details B) Measure vertical angles C) Replace the compass D) Fix station elevation
**Answer: A.** Basis: standard function of a tie line.

**Q9.** The actual length of a 20 m chain is found to be 20.05 m. A line recorded as 300 m is actually:
A) 299.25 m B) 300.05 m C) 300.75 m D) 301.50 m
**Answer: C.** Basis: classic chain-correction numerical — true length = 300 × (20.05/20) = 300.75 m.

**Q10.** Error due to an incorrect chain length is:
A) Compensating B) Cumulative C) Mistake D) Accidental
**Answer: B.** Basis: standard error classification — wrong chain length always acts in one direction.

**Q11.** Fore bearing and back bearing of a line differ by exactly 180° when:
A) Local attraction is present B) No local attraction exists C) The line runs north D) At the magnetic pole
**Answer: B.** Basis: fundamental compass-check rule.

**Q12.** Local attraction in compass surveying is detected by:
A) Difference of FB and BB ≠ 180° B) FB = BB C) Zero declination D) Needle pointing south
**Answer: A.** Basis: standard field test for local attraction.

**Q13.** The whole circle bearing of a line is 135°. Its reduced bearing is:
A) N45°E B) S45°E C) S45°W D) N45°W
**Answer: B.** Basis: routine WCB→RB conversion numerical — 180° − 135° = S45°E.

**Q14.** FB of a line is 145°. Its WCB back bearing is:
A) 35° B) 215° C) 325° D) 145°
**Answer: C.** Basis: BB = FB + 180° (since 145° < 180°) → 325°.

**Q15.** Magnetic declination is the angle between:
A) Magnetic meridian and true meridian B) Geographical meridians of two places C) True bearing and azimuth D) Needle and horizontal plane
**Answer: A.** Basis: standard definition (needle–horizontal-plane angle is dip/inclination, a frequent distractor).

**Q16.** In the Bowditch (compass) rule, the corrections to latitude and departure of each traverse side are proportional to:
A) The length of the side B) The bearing of the side C) The angle at the station D) The coordinates
**Answer: A.** Basis: classic traverse-balancing rule; transit rule (proportional to coordinates) is the usual distractor.

**Q17.** Temporary adjustments of a dumpy level include:
A) Axis alignment, collimation test B) Levelling up, focusing, elimination of parallax C) Centering, levelling, focusing D) Two-peg test only
**Answer: B.** Basis: standard instrumentation question; permanent adjustments are a separate list.

**Q18.** The collimation error of a level is detected by the:
A) Needle test B) Two-peg test C) Convergence test D) Closing test
**Answer: B.** Basis: classic test–instrument pairing.

**Q19.** The R.L. of a benchmark is 100.000 m. B.S. = 1.560 m, F.S. = 2.340 m. The R.L. of the station is:
A) 100.780 m B) 99.220 m C) 98.880 m D) 103.900 m
**Answer: B.** Basis: fundamental levelling arithmetic — 100.000 + 1.560 − 2.340 = 99.220 m.

**Q20.** The combined correction for curvature and refraction in levelling (D in km) is approximately:
A) 0.0673 D² B) 0.0112 D² C) 0.0562 D² D) 0.1176 D²
**Answer: C.** Basis: standard formula repeatedly asked; curvature −0.0673D², refraction +0.0112D², net −0.0562D² (subtracted from staff reading).

**Q21.** Reciprocal levelling eliminates the effect of:
A) Curvature only B) Refraction only C) Collimation, curvature and refraction D) Local attraction
**Answer: C.** Basis: standard reason for using reciprocal levelling across obstacles.

**Q22.** For a given terrain, the contour interval chosen should be:
A) Larger for larger map scale B) Smaller for larger map scale C) Unrelated to scale D) Always 1 m
**Answer: B.** Basis: standard contouring principle — more detailed (large-scale) maps need smaller intervals.

**Q23.** A closed contour with higher values inside represents:
A) A depression B) A hill C) A pond D) A saddle
**Answer: B.** Basis: classic contour-interpretation question; lower-inside means depression.

### B. Karnataka Land Revenue Act & Land Records (4 Q)

**Q24.** The Karnataka Land Revenue Act was enacted in the year:
A) 1956 B) 1961 C) 1964 D) 1972
**Answer: C.** Basis: standard Act-year question; Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 (Land Reforms Act is 1961 — a deliberate distractor pair).

**Q25.** "RTC" in Karnataka land records stands for:
A) Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops B) Register of Title and Cadastre C) Revenue Total Computerised Register D) Record of Tenure Certificate
**Answer: A.** Basis: the single most job-relevant document for a Land Surveyor post.

**Q26.** The record that shows soil classification, land use, area of each survey number and water sources is:
A) Khata B) RTC (Pahani) C) Tanakh / A-Book D) Immovable Property Register
**Answer: C.** Basis: document-function matching, typical of DLRS-recruitment questions. (Khata = assessment/holding details; RTC = possession + crops.)

**Q27.** The correct order of Karnataka land-administration hierarchy from below is:
A) Village → Taluk → Hobli → District B) Village → Hobli → Taluk → District C) Hobli → Village → District → Taluk D) Taluk → Hobli → Village → District
**Answer: B.** Basis: standard administrative-structure question in Karnataka revenue exams.

### D. Mental Ability / Reasoning (9 Q)

**Q43.** Number series: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
A) 35 B) 36 C) 37 D) 38
**Answer: C.** Basis: classic n²+1 pattern (6² + 1 = 37).

**Q44.** Letter series: AZ, BY, CX, DW, ?
A) EV B) FV C) EU D) FW
**Answer: A.** Basis: standard series logic — first letter advances, second letter retraces.

**Q45.** The present ages of A and B are in the ratio 3 : 5. Four years hence the ratio will be 5 : 8. B's present age is:
A) 48 years B) 56 years C) 60 years D) 64 years
**Answer: C.** Basis: standard ratio-age type; 3x+4/5x+4 = 5/8 ⇒ x = 12 ⇒ B = 60.

**Q46.** If EARTH is coded as FBSUI, how is MOON coded?
A) NPPO B) NPOP C) MPPO D) NOPN
**Answer: A.** Basis: classic +1 letter-shift coding.

**Q47.** A man walks 5 km north, turns right and walks 3 km, then turns right and walks 5 km. How far is he from the start and in which direction?
A) 3 km, East B) 3 km, West C) 8 km, East D) 5 km, South
**Answer: A.** Basis: standard direction-sense problem.

**Q48.** Pointing to a man, a woman said, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the man related to the woman?
A) Brother B) Nephew C) Son D) Husband
**Answer: C.** Basis: classic blood-relation puzzle — the "only daughter of my mother" is the speaker herself.

**Q49.** Statements: All roses are flowers. Some flowers fade quickly. Conclusions: (I) Some roses fade quickly. (II) Some flowers are roses.
A) Only I follows B) Only II follows C) Both follow D) Neither follows
**Answer: B.** Basis: standard syllogism — (I) is the classic invalid inference; (II) follows because the roses that are flowers are themselves flowers.

**Q50.** 20% of a number is 45. The number is:
A) 90 B) 180 C) 225 D) 250
**Answer: C.** Basis: basic percentage arithmetic common in reasoning sections.

**Q51.** The angle between the hands of a clock at 3:30 is:
A) 60° B) 75° C) 90° D) 105°
**Answer: B.** Basis: standard clock problem — hour hand at 105°, minute hand at 180°.
