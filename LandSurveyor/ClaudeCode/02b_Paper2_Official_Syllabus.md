## MATHEMATICS (40%)

### ARITHMETIC — Number System

**1. Squares and square roots, cubes and cube roots** — geometrical pattern of square numbers; squares of decimals and fractions, square roots; determination of square root of a perfect square; estimation of sq. root of a non-perfect square; cubes and cube roots; cube root by factor method; identification of sq numbers; square root of perfect sq. by factor method; sq. of perfect sq., decimals and non-perfect numbers; smallest number to add/subtract to make a perfect square; square root by division method.

**2. Set Theory** — representation of a set; types of sets; union, intersection, complement, difference of sets; Venn diagrams; operations of sets; representation using Venn diagrams; properties of union and intersection; De Morgan's laws; relation between number of elements of sets.

**3. Sequence** — definition of sequence and series; meaning and specified terms of AP, GP, HP; sum of terms in AP and GP; relation between AM, GM and HM.

**4. Matrices** — definition, route maps, tabular information; order of matrix; types of matrices; equality of matrices; classification based on elements and order; matrices and transpose; conditions for addition, subtraction and multiplication of matrices.
**5. Permutations and Combinations** — concept; distinguish between permutations and combinations; factorial notation; meanings of ⁿPʳ and ⁿCʳ.

**6. Statistics** — frequency distribution table; graphical/tabular representation, pie chart; mean, measures of central tendency; arithmetic of ungrouped data, median; mean, median and mode for grouped data; range, quartile, deviation and mean deviation; statistical measures; histogram and frequency polygons; collection of data; calculation of mean, median, mode for ungrouped data; bar chart and sector graph; standard deviation (ungrouped and grouped data); coefficient of variation.

**7. H.C.F and L.C.M** — application of HCF; application of HCF in factorization; lowest common multiple.

### ALGEBRA

**1. Basics of Algebra** — basic terms; various algebraic terms; operations with literal signed numbers; use of symbols.

**2. Multiplication of Algebraic Expressions** — multiplication of binomials; special products; squaring of trinomial; expressions (a+b)², (a−b)², (a+b)(a−b), (a+b+c)².

**3. Linear Equations** — solving a linear equation; balance scales; applications; rectangular coordinate systems and graphs; quadrants and convention of signs; graph in linear equations.

**4. Quadratic Equations** — pure quadratic equation; affected quadratic equation by factorization; solving by formula method; reducible to ax²+bx+c=0; problems; nature of roots; relationship between roots and coefficients; forming an equation for given roots; graphical method of solving.

**5. Factorisation of Algebraic Expressions** — of trinomials; using algebraic tiles; of quadratic trinomial ax²+bx+c; factorise a²+2ab+b², a²−2ab+b² and a²−b² using identities; factorisation using standard identities.

**6. HCF & LCM of Binomials and Trinomials** — meaning of HCF and LCM of algebraic expression; finding HCF and LCM of binomials and trinomials.

**7. Co-ordinates — Plane Geometry** — plotting of graph, simple equations; calculating values along one axis given the other.

### GEOMETRY

**1. Axioms and Postulates** — Axioms 1–6, Postulates 1–6; definitions; enunciations (statements); point, ray and lines; linear pair; vertically opposite angles; parallel lines, transversals; corresponding angles when a transversal cuts two parallel lines; lines parallel to the same line.

**2. Theorems** — converse of a theorem; theorems on parallel — pairs of alternate angles, pairs of interior angles.

**3. Polygons** — definition, diagonals and interior regions; identification, inscribing regular polygons; regular vs irregular; inscribe regular pentagon, hexagon and octagon; well-conditioned polygons and their properties.

**4. Triangles** — classification, exterior angles, sum of 3 angles = 180°; proving theorems logically with reference to angles; deductive reasoning; riders; construction of triangles, steps; equilateral and right-angled triangle.

**5. Congruence of Triangles** — meaning; congruency; postulates (SAS, SSS, ASA); right-angle hypotenuse-side (RHS) postulate.

**6. Concurrent Lines of a Triangle** — altitude and its construction; medians; properties of centroid; perpendicular bisectors; circumcircle; angular bisectors; incentre/incircle; steps of construction.

**7. Quadrilaterals** — definition, identification; ten elements of a given quadrilateral; construction to given measurements; area by formula.

**8. Types of Quadrilaterals** — identification of types, consecutive angles; properties of each type; construction of parallelograms, rhombus, trapezia; area of parallelograms, rhombus, trapezium by formula.

**9. Theorems on Parallelograms** — properties; meaning of corollary; corollaries of theorems.

**10. Mid-point Theorem** — definition; prove logically; converse; problems and riders.

**11. Areas** — properties of parallelograms; standing on same base and between same parallels; theorems on areas of parallelograms; problems and riders.

**12. Cyclic Quadrilaterals** — definition, properties, theorems, construction; problems and riders.

**13. Surface Areas and Volumes of Solids** — prism and pyramid: definition, properties, difference; surface area and volume of prisms and pyramids.

**14. Circles** — definition of circle, radius, circumference, diameter, chord, arc, segment; chord property; central and inscribed angle; angle-property theorem; equal chords equidistant from centre; segment construction; concentric and congruent circles; secant and tangents; tangent constructions (at a point, from external point, direct tangent to two circles, transverse common tangents); relation between radius, contact point and tangent; properties of tangents.

**15. Theorems on Triangles and Circles** — concept of similarity; similarity of triangles; basic proportionality (Thales) theorem and corollary; theorems on similar triangles; areas of similar triangles.

**16. Right-Angled Triangle** — Pythagoras theorem; touching circles; distance between centres of touching circles; tangents from an external point.

## PHYSICS (10%)

**1. Magnetism** — natural and artificial magnets; properties; magnetic field and its detection; magnetic substances (non-magnetic, diamagnetic, paramagnetic, ferromagnetic); preparing magnets (mechanical, electrical); uses; Earth's magnetism; magnetic lines of force around a bar magnet; electromagnetism; instruments using the magnetic effect of current.

**2. Electricity** — units of electricity; electric power and its units; practical unit; effects of electric current (magnetic, chemical, heating); electric fuse; electric bulb; sources of electricity (electric cell, dry cell); electromotive force.

**3. Electromagnetic Radiation** — introduction; EM radiation and properties of EM waves; EM spectrum; uses (light, infrared, ultraviolet, X-rays, gamma rays, micro waves, radio waves); Laser and its characteristics.

**4. Electronics** — radio (communication, transmitter, receiver); television.

**5. Dynamics** — motion; distance and displacement; speed, velocity, acceleration; graphic representation; equation of motion; periodic motion; simple pendulum (laws, uses); waves and wave motion; energy — mechanical work, forms and sources; Newton's laws of motion; acceleration due to gravity; equations of motion under gravitational force.

**6. Heat** — nature of heat; heat & temperature; temperature scales; Fahrenheit–Celsius and Celsius–Kelvin relations; measurement (mercury, clinical, max–min thermometers); units of heat; changes of state; effects of heat (thermal and linear expansion).

**7. Light** — importance; refraction, laws, refractive index, effects; total internal reflection and applications (optical instruments, optic fibre, diamond sparkle, bicycle reflector); mirage; refraction in a prism, dispersion; lenses (refraction through lenses, thin lens as prisms, parameters, sign rules, image formation by convex/concave lenses); myopia and hypermetropia; optical instruments (simple/compound microscope, camera, telescope, binoculars); dispersion & spectrum, spectroscope; Raman effect.

**8. Sound** — introduction; ultrasonic sound and uses; SONAR (how it works) and ultrasound scanner; Doppler effect in sound and light and applications.

**9. Gravitation** — introduction; universal law of gravitation; gravity near Earth's surface (weight, weightlessness, variation of g); importance of the law of gravitation.

## GEOGRAPHY (10%)

**1. The Earth** — the universe, shape of the Earth; axis; rotation and revolution; important lines of latitudes and zones; latitudes and longitudes; longitudes and time; zonal time; International Date Line.

**2. The Lithosphere** — layers of the Earth; rocks and weathering of rocks; earthquakes & volcanoes (primary waves, secondary waves, long waves).

**3. Maps** — characteristics of maps; types of maps (large scale / small scale); thematic maps; globes.

**4. Physical Features of India** — location and extent; northern mountains; north Indian plateau; the peninsular plateau; coastal plains and islands; rivers and lakes of India.

---

## MODERN METHODS OF SURVEYING (20%)

**Photogrammetry and Aerial Survey** — introduction, definition, basic principles, methods, importance of scale, height, applications. **Satellite Imagery** — types of satellites (optical, multispectral, hyperspectral, SAR); satellite image acquisition; resolution (spatial, spectral, temporal, radiometric); imagery processing — rectification, orthorectification, mosaicking, classification and information extraction; softwares used (Earth Engine, GIS Image Analyst). **Drone (UAV) Imagery** — types of drones, drone survey planning, Ground Control Points (GCPs), image acquisition; processing — Structure from Motion (SfM), point cloud generation, DEM/DSM generation, Orthomosaic / Ortho-Rectified Image (ORI) generation; softwares used.

**Remote Sensing** — introduction, principle of remote sensing, EMR types, resolutions, types of satellites, types of sensors, LIDAR, visual and digital image processing and its application.

**Global Positioning Systems (GPS)** — definition, principles of GPS and its applications.

**Geographical Information System (GIS)** — introduction and principle of GIS, components of GIS and its applications.

**Advanced Positioning and Satellite Communication Technology in Surveying** — GPS: NAVSTAR GPS architecture, signal structure, trilateration principle, types of errors and corrections. DGPS (Differential GPS): concept, base station and rover configuration, real-time and post-processed differential correction, accuracy levels. GNSS Rovers: RTK (Real Time Kinematic) surveying, CORS (Continuously Operating Reference Station) network — **Karnataka State CORS Network**, NTRIP protocol, field data collection with GNSS Rovers, precision in cadastral surveys. Satellite Communication Technology: GNSS satellite constellations (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, NavIC/IRNSS), signal frequencies, satellite geometry and DOP values. Related softwares: GNSS data processing software, post-processing and network adjustment tools, integration with GIS.

---

## COMPUTER APPLICATIONS (20%)

**i) MS Office — MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint; AutoCAD** — introduction to AutoCAD, all commands, drawing simple figures (triangles, rectangles), text writing, hatching, development of 2D and 3D elements, extrusion, mirror option, etc.; operations using AutoCAD; working with **DXF files** — import, export and editing of cadastral DXF data in AutoCAD.

**ii) GIS Softwares and their Applications** — introduction to GIS software environment; open-source desktop GIS: installation, interface, loading vector and raster data, layer management, attribute table operations, spatial queries, digitizing and editing vector layers, styling and symbology, map layout and printing, plugins and extensions, coordinate reference system (CRS) management; use in cadastral mapping. **GeoServer / Map Server** — open-source GIS servers for publishing OGC web services (WMS, WFS, WCS). **PostGIS** — spatial extension to PostgreSQL for storing and querying geographic data.

**iii) Basics of Software Solutions and Land Records Information Technology** — Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC): how applications are designed, developed, tested and deployed. Web application development concepts, web portals and citizen service portals. Mobile application development for field: offline-first design, geo-tagging, digital signature (eSign/DSC), sync with central server. How spatial and textual data is procured, stored and retrieved: State Data Centre (SDC) / architecture, database storage concepts, data access and retrieval methods. Application Programming Interface (API) — concept of REST (Representational State Transfer) and SOAP (Simple Object Access Protocol) APIs, API Gateway, integration of mobile apps with backend databases and web portals. Web portal design and user experience (UX) fundamentals. Java, JSON, XML, HTML, HTTPS.

*Signed: Commissioner, Survey Settlement and Land Records, Bangalore.*
