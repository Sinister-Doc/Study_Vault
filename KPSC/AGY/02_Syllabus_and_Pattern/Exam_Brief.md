## 1. Overview
The Karnataka Public Service Commission (KPSC) conducts the Gazetted Probationers Examination (KAS) for recruitment to prestigious Karnataka State Civil Services (Group 'A' and Group 'B' posts). The selection process operates strictly under the Karnataka Recruitment of Gazetted Probationers (Appointment by Competitive Examinations) Rules, 1997, and subsequent amendments (2011, 2013, 2014, 2020, 2022).

### Selection Stages
1. **Preliminary Examination (ತಪೂರ್ವ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ)**: Objective Multiple-Choice (OMR-based) screening examination (Total 400 Marks across 2 papers). Ratio for admission to Mains is 1:15 of total vacancies per category.
2. **Main Examination (ಮುಖ್ಯ ಲಿಖಿತ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ)**: Conventional Descriptive written examination comprising:
   - 2 Qualifying Language Papers (Kannada & English, 150 marks each, 35% minimum qualifying threshold).
   - 5 Compulsory Merit Papers (Essay, GS-1, GS-2, GS-3, GS-4, 250 marks each, total 1250 marks).
3. **Personality Test / Interview (ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿತ್ವ ಪರೀಕ್ಷೆ)**: 25 Marks (conducted in a 1:3 ratio based on Mains written merit per Amendment Rules 2022).
4. **Final Selection (ಅಂತಿಮ ಆಯ್ಕೆ)**: Determined on aggregate score of 1275 marks (1250 Mains Written + 25 Interview).

*Negative Marking Rule (Rule 10th Amendment 2013)*: For every question answered incorrectly, 0.25 (one-fourth) of the marks assigned to that question are deducted. Multiple responses to a single question are treated as incorrect. Unanswered questions attract 0 penalty.
