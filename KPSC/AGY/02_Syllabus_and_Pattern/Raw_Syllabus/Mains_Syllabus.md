Source: KPSC Notification No. `KPSCKA/EXA1/EXMF/9/2026-EXAM`, Section 12.6, and Official Syllabi published on `kpsc.kar.nic.in/Syllabus`. Retrieved: 2026-10-13.

## 1. QUALIFYING PAPERS (SSLC First Language Standard - 150 Marks Each - 2 Hours)

### Qualifying Kannada (ಕಡ್ಡಾಯ ಕನ್ನಡ)
1. Comprehension of given passages (ಗದ್ಯ ಗ್ರಹಣ) - 25 Marks
2. Précis writing (ಸಂಕ್ಷಿಪ್ತ ಲೇಖನ) - 25 Marks
3. Usage and vocabulary (ಶಬ್ದ ಪ್ರಯೋಗ ಮತ್ತು ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ: ನುಡಿಗಟ್ಟುಗಳು, ಸಮಾನಾರ್ಥಕ, ವಿರುದ್ಧಾರ್ಥಕ, ತತ್ಸಮ-ತದ್ಭವ) - 50 Marks
4. Translation from English to Kannada & Kannada to English (ಭಾಷಾಂತರ) - 25 Marks
5. Short Essay (ಕಿರು ಪ್ರಬಂಧ) - 25 Marks

### Qualifying English
1. Comprehension of given passages - 25 Marks
2. Précis writing - 25 Marks
3. Usage and vocabulary (Vocabulary, Idioms, Phrases, Sentence correction) - 50 Marks
4. Translation from Kannada to English & English to Kannada - 25 Marks
5. Short Essay - 25 Marks

## 2. COMPULSORY MERIT PAPERS (Degree Standard - 250 Marks Each - 3 Hours)

### PAPER – I: ESSAY (ಪ್ರಬಂಧ) - 250 Marks
Two descriptive essays to be written in the medium chosen by the candidate (entirely English or entirely Kannada):
- **Section I (125 Marks)**: Essay on National and International issues (Political, economic, socio-cultural, philosophical, strategic, environmental).
- **Section II (125 Marks)**: Essay on State of Karnataka or Regional / Local issues (Governance, decentralization, culture, regional economic growth, farmer welfare, water and interstate issues).

### PAPER – II: GENERAL STUDIES – 1 (ಇತಿಹಾಸ, ಸಮಾಜ, ಅರ್ಥವ್ಯವಸ್ಥೆ) - 250 Marks
- **Section I: History and Cultural Heritage (India & Karnataka)**
  - Cultural heritage of India: Indus Valley civilization, Vedic period, heterodox religions (Buddhism, Jainism), art, architecture, literature, philosophical systems.
  - Dynasties of Karnataka: Kadambas, Gangas, Chalukyas of Badami and Kalyana, Rashtrakutas, Hoysalas - administrative systems, architecture, and cultural contributions.
  - Vijayanagara Empire, Bahmani Sultanate, Adil Shahis of Bijapur, Nayakas of Keladi, Kingdom of Mysore (Wodeyars, Hyder Ali, Tipu Sultan).
  - Modern Indian history from the 19th century: British colonial rule, socio-religious reform movements, Indian national movement.
  - Freedom struggle and armed resistance in Karnataka: Kittur Rani Chennamma, Sangolli Rayanna, Halagali Bedas, 1857 in Karnataka, Belagavi Congress Session (1924), Vidurashwatha, Shivapura Dhwaja Satyagraha, Mysore Chalo.
  - Karnataka Unification Movement (ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ಏಕೀಕರಣ): Alur Venkata Rao, role of Kannada Sahitya Parishat, Fazal Ali Commission, 1956 integration, naming of Karnataka (1973). Post-independence social movements (Dalit, farmer, backward class movements).
- **Section II: Social and Political Perspective**
  - Post-independent India: Partition, integration of princely states, linguistic reorganization, nation-building, social changes.
  - Indian Social System: Caste, class, joint family, marriage, religion, modernization, secularization.
  - Rural social system and rural development: Agrarian relations, land reforms in Karnataka (Devaraj Urs era), rural institutions.
  - Social problems and weaker sections: Caste discrimination, untouchability, gender disparity, tribal development, reservations, welfare measures for SC/ST, OBC, minorities, women, and children.
- **Section III: Indian Economy – Planning – Rural Development & Data Analysis**
  - Indian economic structure: Growth trends, sectoral contributions, LPG reforms (1991), fiscal and monetary policies, banking, inflation.
  - Economic planning: Five-year plans, NITI Aayog framework, resource mobilization, GST regime.
  - Rural development: Poverty alleviation strategies, employment generation schemes (MGNREGA), rural infrastructure, microfinance, self-help groups (SHGs).
  - Data collection, analysis and interpretation: Frequency distribution, measures of central tendency (mean, median, mode), dispersion, graphical representations, data sufficiency.

### PAPER – III: GENERAL STUDIES – 2 (ಭೂಗೋಳ, ಸಂವಿಧಾನ, ಆಡಳಿತ) - 250 Marks
- **Section I: Physical Features and Natural Resources (World, India & Karnataka)**
  - Physical geography: Geomorphology (earth's crust, plate tectonics, landforms), Climatology (atmosphere, monsoon mechanisms, cyclones), Oceanography (currents, tides, ocean resources).
  - Geography of India: Physiographic divisions, drainage systems, climate, natural vegetation, soils, mineral and power resources.
  - Geography of Karnataka: Physiographic divisions (Coastal plain, Malnad, Northern Maidan, Southern Maidan), major river systems (Krishna, Cauvery, Godavari basins, west-flowing rivers), climate, soils, agriculture, irrigation projects, mineral distribution, forest cover.
  - Natural hazards, disasters and environmental management: Floods, droughts in Karnataka, disaster management framework.
- **Section II: Overview of Indian Constitution & Governance**
  - Indian Constitution: Philosophical foundation, Preamble, Fundamental Rights, Directive Principles of State Policy, Fundamental Duties, Constitutional amendments.
  - Institutional machinery: Union Executive, Parliament, State Executive (Governor, CM, Council of Ministers), State Legislature, Judiciary (Supreme Court, High Courts, Judicial Review, Public Interest Litigation).
  - Federalism: Center-State legislative, administrative and financial relations, Inter-State councils, Finance Commission, Emergency provisions.
  - Democratic decentralization: 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendment Acts, Panchayat Raj system in Karnataka, Karnataka Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Raj Act, 1993, Urban Local Bodies.
- **Section III: Public Administration, Good Governance & International Relations**
  - Public Administration: Meaning, scope, theories, bureaucratic model, civil service in democracy, administrative ethics.
  - Good Governance: Transparency, accountability, Citizens' Charters, Right to Information (RTI Act, 2005), e-Governance initiatives in Karnataka (Sakala, KSWAN, Bhoomi, Seva Sindhu).
  - Anti-corruption institutions: Lokpal, Karnataka Lokayukta Act, Central Vigilance Commission.
  - India’s Foreign Policy: Basic principles, non-alignment, Act East, neighborhood first, relations with major powers and neighbors, global multilateral bodies (UN, G20, BRICS, SCO, WTO).

### PAPER – IV: GENERAL STUDIES – 3 (ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ, ತಂತ್ರಜ್ಞಾನ, ಪರಿಸರ, ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ಆರ್ಥಿಕತೆ) - 250 Marks
- **Section I: Science and Technology in National Development**
  - Nature and scope of Science & Technology, national science policies, premiere research institutions (DRDO, CSIR, ISRO, BARC, IISc).
  - Space technology: Launch vehicles (PSLV, GSLV, LVM3), satellite missions (INSAT, IRS, NavIC), exploration missions (Chandrayaan, Gaganyaan, Aditya-L1).
  - Information Technology & Artificial Intelligence: Digital India, cyber security, AI, quantum computing, Karnataka IT/BT ecosystem.
  - Energy technology: Nuclear power programme, renewable energy (solar, wind, Pavagada solar park), energy transition.
- **Section II: Environment, Ecology & Public Health**
  - Ecology and ecosystem dynamics: Food chains, biogeochemical cycles, ecological succession.
  - Biodiversity: Hotspots, Western Ghats biodiversity, wildlife protection, Protected Area network in Karnataka (Bandipur, Nagarhole, Kudremukh, Anshi, Bannerghatta).
  - Climate change and pollution: Global warming, greenhouse effect, UNFCCC COP negotiations, national and state action plans on climate change, pollution abatement (air, water, solid waste).
  - Public health and hygiene: Communicable and non-communicable diseases, national health programmes, health infrastructure in Karnataka, malnutrition, maternal and child healthcare.
- **Section III: Karnataka Economy, Agriculture & Infrastructure**
  - Agriculture in Karnataka: Cropping patterns, agro-climatic zones, dryland farming, agricultural marketing, APMCs, farm credit.
  - Economy of Karnataka: Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP), sectoral trends, Karnataka State Budget (2026-27), Karnataka Economic Survey (2025-26), fiscal health.
  - Flagship welfare policies: Five Guarantee Schemes (Gruha Lakshmi, Gruha Jyothi, Yuva Nidhi, Shakti, Anna Bhagya), industrial policy of Karnataka, infrastructure corridors, SEZs, urban development.

### PAPER – V: GENERAL STUDIES – 4 (ನೀತಿಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ, ಸಮಗ್ರತೆ, ಮನೋಪ್ರವೃತ್ತಿ) - 250 Marks
- **Section I: Ethics and Human Interface**
  - Ethics: Essence, determinants and consequences of ethics in human actions; dimensions of ethics; ethics in private and public relationships.
  - Human Values: Role of family, society, and educational institutions in inculcating values; lessons from the lives and teachings of great leaders, reformers, and administrators (Basaveshwara, Kanakadasa, Swami Vivekananda, Mahatma Gandhi, Dr. B.R. Ambedkar).
- **Section II: Attitude, Aptitude & Emotional Intelligence**
  - Attitude: Content, structure, function, influence and relation with thought and behavior; moral and political attitudes; social influence and persuasion.
  - Aptitude and foundational values for Civil Service: Integrity, impartiality, objectivity, non-partisanship, dedication to public service, empathy, tolerance, and compassion towards weaker sections.
  - Emotional Intelligence: Concepts, utilities, and application in governance and administration.
- **Section III: Ethics in Public Administration & Case Studies**
  - Ethical dilemmas in public administration, laws, rules, regulations, and conscience as sources of ethical guidance; accountability and ethical governance.
  - Probity in Governance: Concept of public service, philosophical basis of governance and probity, code of ethics, code of conduct, citizen's charter, work culture, quality of service delivery, utilization of public funds, challenges of corruption.
  - Case Studies: Detailed practical scenarios covering administrative corruption, conflict of interest, disaster management, mob violence, gender justice, public trust, and ethical decision-making.
