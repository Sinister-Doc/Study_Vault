# Monthly Current Affairs & GK Master Digest: May 2026 (ಮಾಸಿಕ ಪ್ರಚಲಿತ ಘಟನೆಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಜ್ಞಾನ: ಮೇ 2026)

> [!summary]
> **Executive Summary & High-Yield Examination Anchor**:
> - **Karnataka State Highlight**: Phase 2 of Lok Sabha voting completed across 14 Northern Karnataka parliamentary constituencies on May 7; severe pre-monsoon convective thunderstorms bring localized respite from acute summer heat.

## 1. Karnataka State Polity, Administration & Governance
1. **Phase 2 Lok Sabha Polling in Karnataka (May 7, 2024)**:
   - Polling concluded across remaining 14 parliamentary constituencies in Northern and Central Karnataka: Chikkodi, Belagavi, Bagalkote, Vijayapura, Kalaburagi, Raichur, Bidar, Koppal, Ballari, Haveri, Dharwad, Uttara Kannada, Davanagere, and Shivamogga.
   - Recorded a voter turnout of **71.84%**, with Chikkodi registering the highest turnout (>78.6%) and Kalaburagi recording ~62.2%.
2. **Kittur Karnataka & Kalyana Karnataka Administration Review**:
   - Regional Commissioners held comprehensive monsoon-readiness reviews at Belagavi and Kalaburagi to prepare contingency plans for potential flood inflows from Koyna and Ujjani reservoirs in Maharashtra.
3. **Panchayat Raj & Rural Development Directives**:
   - RDPR Department mandated the desiltation of 50,000 open wells and historical *Kalyanis* across Gram Panchayats under the Mahatma Gandhi NREGS prior to the onset of the South-West monsoon.

## 2. Karnataka Schemes, Welfare Programs & Budget/Finance
1. **One-Year Milestone of 'Five Guarantees' Commemoration**:
   - State Government marked the first anniversary of the launch of the *Pancha Guarantee Yojane*, releasing an official statistical compendium:
     - Over **1.18 crore women** receiving monthly ₹2,000 under Gruha Lakshmi.
     - Over **1.56 crore households** availing zero-bill electricity under Gruha Jyothi.
     - Over **200 crore free trips** availed by women across Karnataka RTC buses under Shakti.
2. **Kalyana Karnataka Education Infrastructure Influx**:
   - KKRDB sanctioned ₹650 Crore for constructing modern pre-university composite college complexes and smart science labs across 41 backward taluks under Article 371J.

## 3. Karnataka Economy, Industry, Infrastructure & Agriculture

## 4. Karnataka Environment, Ecology, Forests & Water Resources
1. **Pre-Monsoon Thunderstorms & Urban Water Inundation in Bengaluru**:
   - Heavy localized convective rainfall (80-100 mm in 2 hours) caused flash inundation in low-lying areas of Bengaluru (Manyata Tech Park, Silk Board, Ecospace), highlighting the urgent requirement for clearing stormwater drain (*Rajakaluve*) buffer encroachments.
2. **Almatti and Narayanpur Dam Storage Levels**:
   - Storage levels in Lal Bahadur Shastri reservoir (Almatti) reached near dead-storage (505 metres against FRL of 519.6 m), halting summer power generation and prioritizing drinking water supply to Bagalkote and Vijayapura cities.

## 6. National Polity, Constitution, Governance & Judicial Rulings
1. **Supreme Court Grants Interim Bail to Sitting Chief Minister**:
   - In a historic ruling, the Supreme Court granted interim bail to Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal until June 1, 2024, specifically to participate in election campaigning for the 18th Lok Sabha general elections, balancing personal liberty, rule of law, and democratic participation.
2. **Calcutta High Court Strikes Down 2010 OBC Certificates**:

## 7. National Economy, Banking, Fiscal Policy & Trade
1. **RBI Dividend Transfer of ₹2.11 Lakh Crore to Government**:
   - The Central Board of the Reserve Bank of India approved the transfer of a record surplus dividend of **₹2,10,874 Crore** to the Central Government for the accounting year 2023-24 (more than double the budgeted estimate of ₹1.02 lakh crore), driven by high interest income on domestic assets and foreign exchange holdings.
   - Raised the Contingency Risk Buffer (CRB) to **6.50%** (within the 5.50-6.50% range recommended by the Bimal Jalan Committee).
2. **S&P Upgrades India's Sovereign Rating Outlook**:
   - Global rating agency S&P Global Ratings revised its sovereign rating outlook for India from **'Stable' to 'Positive'**, affirming the 'BBB-' investment rating on robust economic growth and improved quality of government capital expenditure.
3. **BSE Market Capitalisation Crosses $5 Trillion**:
   - The combined market capitalisation of all companies listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) crossed the historic milestone of **$5 Trillion (₹415 lakh crore)** for the first time in history.

## 9. National Security, Defence & Strategic Affairs
1. **DRDO Test-Fires Next-Gen SMART System**:
   - Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) successfully test-fired the **Supersonic Missile-Assisted Release of Torpedo (SMART)** system from Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Island off the coast of Odisha.
   - Missile-based stand-off torpedo delivery system designed to enhance the Indian Navy's anti-submarine warfare (ASW) capability far beyond the conventional range of lightweight torpedoes.
2. **Indian Navy Deployment in Gulf of Aden / Red Sea**:
   - Western Naval Command warships (*INS Kochi, INS Kolkata*) maintained continuous escort operations, successfully escorting merchant vessels and neutralizing maritime piracy and drone threats under *Operation Sankalp*.
3. **Indo-French Naval Exercise VARUNA 2024 Preparations**:
   - Planning conferences completed for bilateral carrier-strike group maneuvers in the Western Indian Ocean.

   - Made landfall on May 26 near Khepupara (Bangladesh) and Sagar Island (West Bengal) with wind speeds of 110-120 km/h, causing extensive storm surges and flooding in the Sundarbans.

## 11. Science, Technology, Space & IT
1. **Agnikul Cosmos Launches Agnibaan SOrTeD**:
   - Chennai-based space tech startup **Agnikul Cosmos** successfully launched **Agnibaan SOrTeD** (Sub-Orbital Technology Demonstrator) from India's first private launchpad (Dhanush) at Sriharikota.
   - Historic milestone: World's first flight powered by a **patented single-piece 3D-printed semi-cryogenic engine (Agnilet)** using liquid oxygen and aviation turbine fuel.
2. **China's Chang'e 6 Moon Mission**:
   - China launched the Chang'e 6 lunar probe aboard Long March 5 from Wenchang, targeting the first-ever sample return from the lunar far side (South Pole-Aitken Basin).
3. **AI Safety Seoul Summit**:
   - Co-hosted by South Korea and the United Kingdom; global leaders and leading AI companies agreed on the "Seoul Declaration" establishing commitments to test Frontier AI models against safety thresholds.

## 12. International Relations, Treaties, Summits & Geopolitics
1. **India - Iran 10-Year Long-Term Contract on Chabahar Port**:
   - Signed on May 13, 2024, in Tehran between India Ports Global Limited (IPGL) and the Ports and Maritime Organisation (PMO) of Iran.
   - Grants India operational rights over the **Shahid Beheshti Terminal at Chabahar Port for 10 years**.
   - IPGL will invest **$120 Million** in port equipment and facilitate a $250 Million credit line for infrastructure development.
   - Strategic Importance: Bypasses Pakistan to provide India landlocked access to Afghanistan, Central Asian Republics (CAR), and links with the International North-South Transport Corridor (INSTC).
2. **Three European Nations Recognize State of Palestine**:
   - **Spain, Ireland, and Norway** formally recognized Palestine as an independent state on May 28, 2024, urging a two-state solution to resolve the Gaza conflict.
3. **International Court of Justice (ICJ) Order on Rafah**:
   - The ICJ issued legally binding provisional measures ordering Israel to immediately halt its military offensive in Rafah, southern Gaza, and keep the Rafah crossing open for unhindered humanitarian aid.

## 16. Prelims Traps & Mains Answer Frameworks

### 16.1 Prelims Traps
> [!caution]
> - **Trap 1**: Confusing the terminal operated by India at Chabahar Port.
>   - *Fact*: India operates the **Shahid Beheshti Terminal**, NOT the Shahid Kalantari terminal.
> - **Trap 2**: Believing Agnibaan SOrTeD was launched from a Government ISRO launchpad.
>   - *Fact*: It was launched from **Dhanush**, India's first *privately owned launchpad* established by Agnikul Cosmos at SDSC SHAR, Sriharikota.
> - **Trap 3**: Confusing the naming country for Cyclone Remal.
>   - *Fact*: The name 'Remal' was contributed by **Oman** (meaning sand in Arabic), NOT Bangladesh, India, or Pakistan.
> - **Trap 4**: Assuming the RBI dividend transfer to the government violated Bimal Jalan norms.
>   - *Fact*: The transfer retained the Contingency Risk Buffer at **6.50%**, strictly at the upper boundary of the recommended 5.50-6.50% band.

### 16.2 Mains Question & Model Blueprint
**Question**: *Analyze the geopolitical and geo-economic significance of the 10-year Chabahar Port agreement for India. What diplomatic hurdles and sanctions risks does India face in operationalizing this maritime corridor?* (Marks: 15 / 250 Words)

**Model Answer Outline**:
1. **Introduction (35 words)**:
   - Introduce the long-term 10-year contract signed in May 2024 between India and Iran for Shahid Beheshti terminal at Chabahar Port as a decisive geopolitical breakthrough securing India's western continental connectivity without transit dependency on Pakistan.
2. **Body 1: Geo-economic and Strategic Dividend (75 words)**:
   - *Gateway to Landlocked Central Asia*: Provides unhindered maritime-rail connectivity to Afghanistan, Uzbekistan, and Kazakhstan, tapping into vast mineral, uranium, and hydrocarbon reserves.
   - *INSTC Integration*: Anchors the International North-South Transport Corridor, cutting container transit time to Russia and Europe by 40% and costs by 30% compared to the Suez Canal route.
   - *Strategic Counter-weight*: Serves as a vital counterbalance to China's deep-sea commercial footprint at Gwadar Port (just 72 nautical miles away) under the China-Pakistan Economic Corridor (CPEC).
3. **Body 2: Diplomatic Hurdles and US Sanctions Risks (75 words)**:
   - *Countering America's Adversaries Through Sanctions Act (CAATSA)*: Risk of secondary sanctions from the United States targeting Indian port operators and logistics firms engaging with Iranian state entities.
   - *Regional Geopolitical Turbulence*: The death of Iranian President Ebrahim Raisi, domestic political transitions in Tehran, and ongoing Israel-Iran military frictions pose continuous operating risks.
   - *Infrastructure Deficits*: Delays in completing the Chabahar-Zahedan railway line impede seamless modal transfer from container ship berths to freight wagons.
4. **Conclusion (25 words)**:
   - Conclude that India must leverage its strategic partnership with Washington to negotiate formal sanctions waivers, while accelerating rail logistics to transform Chabahar into a thriving multi-modal trade artery.
