## 3. Karnataka Economy, Industry, Infrastructure & Agriculture
1. **Tungabhadra Dam Crest Gate No. 19 Crisis & Emergency Restoration**:
   - On the night of August 10, the chain link of **Crest Gate No. 19** of the Tungabhadra Dam at Hosapete (Vijayanagara district) snapped under high reservoir hydrostatic pressure (105 TMC storage), leading to an uncontrolled discharge of over 35,000 cusecs.
   - Dam safety authorities were forced to open all 33 crest gates, releasing over 1.5 lakh cusecs into the downstream river bed to lower water levels and prevent structural vibrations.
   - Under the guidance of veteran dam design expert **N. Kannaiah Naidu**, engineering teams fabricated and installed a **5-tier Stop-Log Barrier (ಗೇಟ್ ಸ್ಟಾಪ್ ಲಾಗ್)** within 6 days, successfully plugging the breach and saving over 30 TMC of irrigation water for crops in Ballari, Vijayanagara, Koppal, and Raichur.
2. **KRS & Kabini Flood Discharge Operations**:
   - Continued heavy rainfall in the Western Ghats forced KRS authorities to maintain peak discharges exceeding 80,000 cusecs, filling downstream tanks and recharging cascading aquifers in Mandya and Mysuru districts.
3. **Bengaluru-Mysuru Expressway Toll Operations**:
   - National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) implemented automated AI-enabled speed tracking cameras to curb over-speeding and accidents along the 118-km expressway.

## 6. National Polity, Constitution, Governance & Judicial Rulings
1. **Supreme Court Upholds SC/ST Sub-Classification (Davinder Singh Case)**:
   - *Case*: *State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh & Ors.* (7-Judge Constitution Bench).
   - *Verdict*: By a landmark 6-1 majority (Justice Bela M. Trivedi dissenting), the Supreme Court **overruled the 2004 5-judge bench decision in E.V. Chinnaiah v. State of Andhra Pradesh**.
   - *Key Legal Principles Established*:
     1. **Article 341 does not create a monolithic caste identity**: States have the legislative competence under **Article 15(4) and Article 16(4)** to sub-classify Scheduled Castes to provide internal reservation to more marginalized sub-castes.
     2. **Empirical Data Requirement**: Sub-classification cannot be arbitrary or politically motivated; it must be justified by demonstrable empirical evidence of unequal representation and backwardness.
     3. **No 100% Reservation**: States cannot allocate 100% of the quota to a single sub-group.
     4. **Creamy Layer Application**: Four of the six majority judges (Justices B.R. Gavai, Vikram Nath, Pankaj Mithal, and Satish Chandra Sharma) held that the state must identify and exclude the **Creamy Layer** among SCs and STs to ensure equitable distribution of affirmative action.
2. **Waqf (Amendment) Bill, 2024 Introduced**:
   - Ministry of Minority Affairs introduced the Waqf (Amendment) Bill in the Lok Sabha to amend the Waqf Act, 1995, proposing inclusion of non-Muslim members and women in Waqf Boards and establishing judicial oversight on declaration of properties as *waqf by user*. Referred to a Joint Parliamentary Committee (JPC) headed by Jagdambika Pal.

## 7. National Economy, Banking, Fiscal Policy & Trade
1. **Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) Meeting**:
   - RBI kept the Repo Rate steady at **6.50%** for the 9th consecutive bi-monthly meeting, noting that while food inflation remains elevated (at 8.36%), core inflation has moderated to historical lows (~3.1%).
2. **Unified Pension Scheme (UPS) Approved by Union Cabinet**:
   - On August 24, 2024, the Union Cabinet approved the **Unified Pension Scheme (UPS)** for central government employees (effective April 1, 2025), balancing the fiscal sustainability of the National Pension System (NPS) with the social security of the Old Pension Scheme (OPS).
   - *Core Pillars*:
     - **Assured Pension**: 50% of the average basic pay drawn over the last 12 months prior to superannuation for a minimum qualifying service of 25 years.
     - **Assured Family Pension**: 60% of the pension of the employee in the event of demise.
     - **Assured Minimum Pension**: ₹10,000 per month on superannuation after minimum 10 years of service.
     - **Inflation Indexation**: Dearness Relief (DR) aligned with All India Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers (AICPI-IW).
     - **Government Contribution Increased**: From 14% to **18.5%** of basic pay + DA (employee contribution unchanged at 10%).

## 9. National Security, Defence & Strategic Affairs
1. **Commissioning of INS Arighat (SSBN)**:
   - On August 29, 2024, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh formally commissioned **INS Arighat (S3)**, India's second indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine, into the Indian Navy at Ship Building Centre (SBC), Visakhapatnam.
   - Follows lead ship **INS Arihant (S2)**; constructed under the secret Advanced Technology Vessel (ATV) programme.
   - Powered by an 83 MW pressurised light-water reactor using enriched uranium; equipped with four vertical launch tubes capable of carrying up to twelve K-15 *Sagarika* SLBMs (range ~750 km) or four K-4 SLBMs (range ~3,500 km).
   - Provides an invulnerable second-strike nuclear retaliatory capability, completing India's sea-based continuous-at-sea-deterrence (CASD).
2. **Exercise Tarang Shakti 2024 (Phase 1 at Sulur)**:
   - Indian Air Force conducted Phase 1 of its largest multilateral air combat exercise *Tarang Shakti* at Sulur Air Force Station, Tamil Nadu, featuring participation from the German Luftwaffe, French Air and Space Force, Spanish Air Force, and UK Royal Air Force.
3. **PM Modi Visits Ukraine (First by an Indian PM)**:
   - On August 23, 2024, Narendra Modi made a historic visit to Kyiv, becoming the **first Indian Prime Minister to visit Ukraine** since bilateral ties were established in 1992. Held summit talks with President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, reiterating India's principled stance that "no problem can be solved on the battlefield" and advocating dialogue and diplomacy.

## 16. Prelims Traps & Mains Answer Frameworks

### 16.1 Prelims Traps
> [!caution]
> - **Trap 1**: Believing the Supreme Court sub-classification verdict permits 100% reservation for a sub-caste.
>   - *Fact*: The Supreme Court explicitly ruled that the state **CANNOT allocate 100% of the quota to a single sub-group**, which would violate the equality code.
> - **Trap 2**: Confusing the reactor type and fuel of INS Arighat.
>   - *Fact*: INS Arighat is powered by an **83 MW Pressurised Light Water Reactor (PWR) using enriched uranium**, NOT heavy water or thorium.
> - **Trap 3**: Believing Manu Bhaker won a Gold medal in Paris 2024.
>   - *Fact*: Manu Bhaker won **two Bronze medals** (Women's 10m Air Pistol and 10m Air Pistol Mixed Team). India won zero gold medals in Paris 2024.
> - **Trap 4**: Misattributing the SSLV-D3 launcher status.
>   - *Fact*: SSLV-D3 was the **third and final developmental flight**, successfully completing developmental trials and transitioning the rocket to operational commercial status.

### 16.2 Mains Question & Model Blueprint
**Question**: *Examine the constitutional validity and socio-economic ramifications of the Supreme Court's ruling permitting internal sub-classification of Scheduled Castes in State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh. How does the exclusion of the 'creamy layer' reconcile substantive equality with social justice?* (Marks: 15 / 250 Words)

**Model Answer Outline**:
1. **Introduction (35 words)**:
   - Frame the 7-judge Constitution Bench verdict in *State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh (2024)* as a landmark recalibration of affirmative action jurisprudence, overturning the rigid *E.V. Chinnaiah* doctrine to operationalize substantive equality (*as equals must be treated equally, and unequals unequally*).
2. **Body 1: Constitutional Validity & Legal Tenets (70 words)**:
   - *Harmonizing Art 341 with Art 15(4) & 16(4)*: Art 341 merely designates castes into the Presidential List for administrative identification; it does not confer a homogeneous socio-economic status or strip states of their power under Art 16(4) to provide preferential quotas to more disadvantaged sub-castes.
   - *Empirical Data as a Constitutional Guardrail*: The court mandated that sub-classification cannot be an exercise in arbitrary political patronage; it requires verifiable empirical data proving severe under-representation in state public employment.
   - *Prohibition of Monopoly*: States cannot exclude other sub-groups entirely by earmarking 100% of seats for a single favored sub-caste.
3. **Body 2: The 'Creamy Layer' Dilemma & Socio-Economic Impact (75 words)**:
   - *Preventing Elite Capture*: Benefits of affirmative action over seven decades have often been monopolized by advanced, urbanized sub-castes within the SC list (e.g., Right group / Chamars), while the most vulnerable (e.g., Left group / Madigas, manual scavengers) remain deprived.
   - *Stigma vs. Economic Backwardness*: Critics argue that caste-based untouchability and social prejudice (*asprishyata*) are experienced irrespective of wealth or class, unlike the OBC criteria established in *Indra Sawhney*.
   - *Administrative Complexity*: Devising objective criteria to determine the SC creamy layer without diluting social protection demands rigorous state commission evaluation.
4. **Conclusion (25 words)**:
   - Conclude that internal sub-classification breathes new life into Dr. B.R. Ambedkar's vision of egalitarian social democracy, provided it is implemented through transparent empirical methodologies and robust administrative integrity.
