### Q1. [Karnataka History | Part A: Fundamental & Conceptual Questions] ID: KAS-SEC-KAR-001
**Question**: Question 1: In the context of Karnataka history and dynasties, examine the historical statement:
Which inscription is considered the earliest documented stone epigraph in the Kannada language?

- **A**: Halmidi Inscription (c. 450 CE) (Correct)
- **B**: Talagunda Pillar Inscription
- **C**: Badami Cliff Epigraph
- **D**: Aihole Prasasti

- **Official Key**: **A**
- **Statement Verification**: Per-statement verified
- **Detailed Explanation**: Halmidi inscription in Belur taluk, Hassan district, under Kadamba King Kakusthavarma, is the earliest Kannada stone epigraph.
- **Topic / Focus**: Sectional
