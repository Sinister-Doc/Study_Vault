# Batched Decisions Log — KEA Village Administrative Officer (VAO) 2026

## Decision Session 1: 2026-09-30

### Decision 1: Paper Structure & Proportional Balancing
- **Context:** KEA VAO is structured into two 100-mark papers: Paper-I (General Knowledge, Rural Administration & Panchayat Raj) and Paper-II (General Kannada 35, General English 35, Computer Knowledge 30).
- **Adopted Resolution:** Paper 2 is the decisive rank differentiator. Notes 01 (Kannada), 02 (English), and 03 (Computers) will receive intense grammatical rules, tables, shortcut charts, and high-frequency PYQs. Paper 1 notes (04 to 07) will rigorously cover Karnataka History, Geography, Polity, and Karnataka Gram Swaraj & Panchayat Raj Act 1993 with land revenue administration duties.

---

### Decision 2: Temporal Window for Current Affairs
- **Adopted Resolution:** 12-month window (October 2025 – September 2026), heavily focused on Karnataka Budget 2026–27 (₹4,48,004 crore), the Five Guarantee Schemes (Gruha Lakshmi, Shakti, Yuva Nidhi, Anna Bhagya, Gruha Jyothi), and state revenue digitization (Bhoomi, Kaveri 2.0, e-Swathu, Dishank).

---

### Decision 3: Mock Test Architecture
- **Adopted Resolution:** Full-Length Mocks: 2 exact 100-question, 120-minute simulations (+1.0 / -0.25). Subject-wise mocks: strictly proportional timing (General Kannada: 35 Qs / 42 mins; General English: 35 Qs / 42 mins; Computer Knowledge: 30 Qs / 36 mins; Rural Admin & RDPR: 25 Qs / 30 mins; Karnataka GK: 25 Qs / 30 mins).
