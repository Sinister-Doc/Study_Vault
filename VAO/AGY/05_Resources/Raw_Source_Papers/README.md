## 2. Provenance & Authority Verification

- **Recruitment Notification:** Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA) Notification for Village Administrative Officer (VAO) Group-C Posts in the Department of Revenue.
- **Examination Dates:** 
  - Compulsory Kannada Language Test: **29 September 2024**
  - Main Competitive Written Exam (Paper 1 & Paper 2): **27 October 2024**
- **Official Publishing Portal:** KEA Official Website (`cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/`) & authorized examination archives (*shikarat.com*).
- **Test Booklet Codes:** Paper 1 Code `C1`, Paper 2 Code `A1` (`VAO2710P224`).

## 3. Syllabus Mapping & Question Verbatim Concordance

- **Paper-I (General Knowledge & Rural Administration):**
  - **Q1:** Modern Indian History / European Trading Companies (Dutch business centres: Pulicat, Surat, Machilipatnam, Nagapattinam) -> `[P1-VAO-HIST-1.1]`.
  - **Q2:** Environmental Governance & Western Ghats (K. Kasturirangan Committee) -> `[P1-VAO-ENV-5.1]`.
  - **Q3:** Ancient Indian History (Harappa Granaries, Mohenjodaro Dancing Girl, Kalibangan Fire Altars, Lothal Dockyard) -> `[P1-VAO-HIST-1.1]`.
  - **Q4:** Indian Constitution (Article 14 Equality before law vs. Articles 21, 24, 30) -> `[P1-VAO-POL-3.1]`.
- **Paper-II (Language & Computer Knowledge):**
  - **Q1–3:** Kannada Phonetics (ಉಚ್ಚಾರಣಾ ಸ್ಥಾನಗಳು: ಕಂಠ್ಯ, ತಾಲವ್ಯ, ಮೂರ್ಧನ್ಯ, ಓಷ್ಠ್ಯ), Nouns (ರೂಢನಾಮ, ಭಾವನಾಮ, ಅಂಕಿತನಾಮ, ದಿಗ್ವಾಚಕ), Verbs & Tenses -> `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.1]`.
  - **Q40–45:** General English (Prepositions, Question Tags, Reported Speech, Prefixes, Homophones/Vocabulary) -> `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.1]`.
  - **Q82–88:** Computer Knowledge (IPv4 addressing & octet limits, Windows file name character limits, Network masquerading, Memory access hierarchy SSD/HDD/RAM/Cache, ROM/EPROM/EEPROM characteristics, OS Kernel, E-R diagram notations) -> `[P2-VAO-COMP-3.1]` to `[P2-VAO-COMP-3.6]`.

All verbatim questions and their official key answers have been archived directly into `05_Resources/PYQ_Archive/` and integrated into the student mock tests.
