## 2. Statutory Acts & Government Regulations

1. **Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 (Act No. 12 of 1964):**
   - *Section 128:* Duty of citizens to report acquisition of land rights.
   - *Section 129:* Preparation and maintenance of record of rights; mutation register; First-In, First-Out (FIFO) disposal of objections.
   - *Section 132 & 133:* Evidentiary presumption of entries in Record of Rights (Pahani).
   - *Duties of Village Accountant (now VAO):* Maintenance of village accounts, crop inspection reports, collection of land revenue, and reporting natural calamities.
2. **Karnataka Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Raj Act, 1993 (Amended 2015/2016):**
   - Three-tier Panchayati Raj structure (Grama Panchayat, Taluk Panchayat, Zilla Panchayat).
   - Eleventh Schedule 29 functional subjects.
   - 50% reservation for women in elected seats and offices of Adhyaksha/Upadhyaksha.
   - Ward Sabha, Gram Sabha, and Social Audit provisions.

## 5. Question Classification & Provenance Standard

To maintain absolute academic honesty and rigor:
- **Verbatim Official PYQs:** Questions transcribed directly from the official KEA 2024 test booklets (`KEA_VAO_Paper_1_Question_Paper_English_27_10_2024.pdf` & `KEA_VAO_Paper_2_Question_Paper_27_10_2024.pdf`). They carry exact question numbers and verified official answers matching KEA's published key (`KEA_VAO_Paper_2_Official_Key_Answer_27_10_2024.pdf`).
- **High-Yield Expected Questions (Model Simulations):** Created to provide comprehensive exam-hall simulation for syllabus topics newly introduced or expanded in the 2026 pattern (e.g., 2026–27 Karnataka Budget allocations, Mukhya Mantri Saura Krishi Yojane, and digital revenue workflows). These are explicitly labeled as model questions to ensure total transparency.
