## 1. History & Heritage (`[P1-VAO-HIST-1.1]` to `1.4`)

### Q1. [Official KEA VAO Exam 27-10-2024, Paper-I, Q1 (C1)]
**English Question:**
Which of the following places were the business centers of Dutch?
- (1) Machilipatnam, Diu, Daman, Surat
- (2) Surat, Agra, Ahmedabad, Bombay
- (3) Pulicat, Pondicherry, Chandranagur, Mahe
- (4) Pulicat, Surat, Machilipatnam, Nagapattinam

**Answer: (4) Pulicat, Surat, Machilipatnam, Nagapattinam**  
*Historical Explanation:* The Dutch East India Company (VOC) established primary trading factories on the Coromandel coast and Gujarat coast: Pulicat (Fort Geldria, principal seat), Surat (1616), Machilipatnam (1605), and Nagapattinam (conquered from Portuguese in 1658). Diu/Daman were Portuguese; Pondicherry, Chandranagore, and Mahe were French.

### Q6. [Official KEA VAO Exam 27-10-2024, Paper-I, Q6 (C1)]
**English Question:**
Identify the correct sequence of the dynasties who ruled Magadha:
- (1) Haryanka — Maurya — Shishunaga — Nanda — Shunga
- (2) Haryanka — Shishunaga — Nanda — Maurya — Shunga
- (3) Shishunaga — Nanda — Maurya — Shunga — Haryanka

**Answer: (2) Haryanka — Shishunaga — Nanda — Maurya — Shunga**

1. **Haryanka Dynasty:** c. 544 BCE (Bimbisara, Ajatashatru)

3. **Nanda Dynasty:** c. 345 BCE (Mahapadma Nanda, Dhana Nanda)
4. **Maurya Dynasty:** c. 322 BCE (Chandragupta Maurya, Ashoka)
5. **Shunga Dynasty:** c. 185 BCE (Pushyamitra Shunga).

## 2. Indian Constitution & Polity (`[P1-VAO-POL-3.1]` to `3.5`)

### Q4. [Official KEA VAO Exam 27-10-2024, Paper-I, Q4 (C1)]
**English Question:**
Identify the correct combination:
- a) Article 14 — Equality before law
- b) Article 21 — Abolition of titles
- c) Article 24 — Abolition of untouchability
- d) Article 30 — Right to freedom of religion

Select the code for the correct answer from the options given below:
- (1) a only
- (2) b only
- (3) c and d only
- (4) d only

**Answer: (1) a only**  
*Constitutional Explanation:*
- **Article 14:** Equality before law and equal protection of laws (**Correct — a only**).
- **Article 18:** Abolition of titles (Article 21 is Protection of life and personal liberty).
- **Article 17:** Abolition of untouchability (Article 24 is Prohibition of employment of children in factories).
- **Articles 25–28:** Right to freedom of religion (Article 30 is Right of minorities to establish and administer educational institutions).

## 3. Rural Administration & Panchayat Raj (`[P1-VAO-RDPR-4.1]` to `4.5`)

### Q7. [Official KEA VAO Exam 27-10-2024, Paper-I, Q7 (C1)]
**English Question:**
Consider the following statements regarding Gram Panchayats and choose the correct answer from the following codes:
- a) A village or a group of villages comes under the jurisdiction of each Gram Panchayat.
- b) The President of Gram Panchayat has the power to sign the cheques related to financial affairs.
- c) Village Administrative Officer (VAO) has to work under Gram Panchayat as per the orders of President of Gram Panchayat.
- d) There exists no reservation for the selection of President and Vice-President of Gram Panchayat.

Options:
- (1) Only a and b are correct
- (2) Only a and d are correct
- (3) Only c and d are correct
- (4) Only b and c are correct

**Answer: (1) Only a and b are correct**  
*Statutory Explanation under Karnataka Gram Swaraj & Panchayat Raj Act, 1993:*
- Statement a is **correct**: Section 4 defines the declaration of a Panchayat area comprising a village or group of villages.
- Statement b is **correct**: Under Section 58 and financial transaction rules, cheques on the Gram Panchayat Fund are jointly signed by the Adhyaksha (President) and Panchayat Development Officer (PDO).
- Statement c is **false**: The Village Administrative Officer (VAO / Village Accountant) is a revenue department official under the Tahsildar / Assistant Commissioner, not a subordinate servant taking orders directly from the GP Adhyaksha.
- Statement d is **false**: Section 44 specifically mandates reservation of offices of Adhyaksha and Upadhyaksha for SCs, STs, Backward Classes, and Women.

## 4. Government Schemes & Environmental Governance (`[P1-VAO-ENV-5.1]`, `[P1-VAO-SCH-6.1]`)

### Q2. [Official KEA VAO Exam 27-10-2024, Paper-I, Q2 (C1)]
**English Question:**
Which of the following statements is/are correct regarding K. Kasturirangan Committee?
- a) The Committee was set up in 2022.
- b) It was set up to study the status of important ecological areas in the Western Ghats.
- c) The Karnataka Government accepted the Kasturirangan Committee report in September 2024.

Options:
- (1) a, b and c are correct
- (2) only c is correct
- (3) only b is correct
- (4) only a and b are correct

**Answer: (3) only b is correct**  
*Current Affairs Explanation:*
- Statement a is false: The High-Level Working Group (HLWG) on Western Ghats headed by Dr. K. Kasturirangan was constituted in **August 2012** (submitted report in 2013).
- Statement b is **correct**: It was appointed to examine the Western Ghats ecology and recommend ecologically sensitive areas (ESAs).
- Statement c is false: The Karnataka Cabinet unanimously **rejected** the implementation of the Kasturirangan Committee report on Western Ghats in September 2024, citing adverse impacts on local livelihoods.

### Q5. [Official KEA VAO Exam 27-10-2024, Paper-I, Q5 (C1)]
**English Question:**
Match the following schemes with their objectives:

| Schemes | Objectives |
| :--- | :--- |
| a) Pradhan Mantri Shram Yogi Maan-dhan Yojana | i. One year life insurance scheme offering coverage for death due to any reason |
| b) Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana | ii. Pension scheme for unorganized workers |
| c) National Social Assistance Programme | iii. One year accidental insurance scheme which provides cover for death or disability |
| d) Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana | iv. Social benefits to poor households in case of old age, death & maternity |

Options:
- (1) a-ii, b-iv, c-i, d-iii
- (2) a-iv, b-i, c-iii, d-ii
- (3) a-ii, b-i, c-iv, d-iii
- (4) a-iii, b-ii, c-i, d-iv

**Answer: (3) a-ii, b-i, c-iv, d-iii**

- **NSAP:** Centrally sponsored scheme providing pensions (IGNOAPS, IGNWPS) and maternity benefits to BPL households (iv).
- **PMSBY:** Renewable one-year accidental death/disability insurance cover of ₹2 lakh (iii).

## 5. General Science & International Organizations

### Q8. [Official KEA VAO Exam 27-10-2024, Paper-I, Q8 (C1)]
**English Question:**
When a gun is fired, gun exerts a force on the bullet and the bullet exerts a force on the gun.  
Statement A: Force on the bullet is equal and opposite to force on the gun.  
Statement B: Total momentum of the bullet and the gun is conserved.  
Statement C: Total kinetic energy of the bullet and the gun is conserved.  
Among the above statements, the correct statement/statements is/are:
- (1) Statement A and Statement B
- (2) Statement B and Statement C
- (3) Statement A and Statement C
- (4) Only Statement C

**Answer: (1) Statement A and Statement B**  
*Physics Explanation:* By Newton's Third Law of Motion, action and reaction are equal and opposite (Statement A is true). Since no external net force acts on the bullet-gun system, linear momentum is conserved (Statement B is true). Kinetic energy is **not** conserved because chemical potential energy from gunpowder is converted into kinetic energy, sound, and thermal energy (inelastic process — Statement C is false).

### Q9. [Official KEA VAO Exam 27-10-2024, Paper-I, Q9 (C1)]
**English Question:**
Which of the following statements is correct with respect to diamond?
- (1) Each carbon atom of diamond is bonded to three other carbon atoms
- (2) Each carbon atom of diamond is bonded to four other carbon atoms
- (3) Each carbon atom of diamond is bonded to six other carbon atoms
- (4) Carbon-Carbon bond does not exist in diamond

**Answer: (2) Each carbon atom of diamond is bonded to four other carbon atoms**  
*Chemistry Explanation:* In diamond, each carbon atom undergoes $sp^3$ hybridization and forms four covalent single bonds directed towards the corners of a regular tetrahedron, giving diamond its extreme hardness and rigid 3D lattice structure. In graphite, each carbon is bonded to three other carbons in planar hexagonal layers.
