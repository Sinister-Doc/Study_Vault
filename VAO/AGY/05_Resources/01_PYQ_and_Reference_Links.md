## 2. Standard Reference Texts & Grammar Manuals

1. **ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಕನ್ನಡ (General Kannada):**
   - *ಕನ್ನಡ ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ ದರ್ಪಣ (Kannada Vyakarana Darpana) — Directorate of Textbooks, Govt. of Karnataka.* Standard authority for Sandhi, Samasa, Vibhakti, and Tatsama-Tadbhav rules.
   - *ಪ್ರೌಢಶಾಲಾ ಕನ್ನಡ ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ — ಬಿ.ಎಂ. ಶ್ರೀಕಂಠಯ್ಯ / ತೀ.ನಂ. ಶ್ರೀಕಂಠಯ್ಯ.*
2. **General English:**
   - *High School English Grammar & Composition — Wren & Martin.* Indispensable for Active/Passive voice, Reported Speech, and Subject-Verb agreement.
   - *Objective General English — S.P. Bakshi (Arihant Publications).*
3. **Computer Knowledge & IT Literacy:**
   - *Computer Awareness — Arihant Experts / Lucent Computer.* Covers hardware architectures, Windows/Linux OS commands, MS Office shortcuts, and internet protocols.
4. **Panchayat Raj & Karnataka Governance:**
   - *Karnataka Panchayat Raj Act, 1993 (with 2015/2020 amendments) — Official Gazette.*
   - *Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 & Rules 1966.*

## 3. Digital Portals & E-Governance Systems in Village Administration

- **Bhoomi (ಭೂಮಿ):** First state-wide computerized land records management system in India. Generates digitally signed **RTC / Pahani (ಫಾರಂ 16)**, mutation register (ಫಾರಂ 12), and Khata extracts.
- **e-Swathu (ಇ-ಸ್ವತ್ತು):** Web application for Gram Panchayats to document rural residential properties. Issues **Form 9 (Building extract)** and **Form 11 (Vacant site extract)**. Eliminates illegal property layouts.
- **Kaveri 2.0 (ಕಾವೇರಿ):** Real-time web-based property registration portal managed by the Department of Stamps and Registration.
- **Parihara (ಪರಿಹಾರ):** Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) portal for agricultural input subsidies and natural disaster relief (drought, flood compensation).
- **e-Kshana:** Instant online caste, income, and residence certificate issuance system integrated with Aadhaar.
