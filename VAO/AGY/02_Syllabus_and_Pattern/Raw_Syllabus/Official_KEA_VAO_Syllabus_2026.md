# Official KEA VAO (Village Administrative Officer) Syllabus 2026 — Verbatim Specification

## I. Examination Architecture & Pattern Matrix

- `[PAT-VAO-01]` **Mode of Examination:** Offline Written Examination (OMR Based).
- `[PAT-VAO-02]` **Total Competitive Papers:** Two Papers (Paper-I & Paper-II).
- `[PAT-VAO-03]` **Total Maximum Marks:** 200 Marks (100 Marks per Paper).
- `[PAT-VAO-04]` **Total Questions:** 200 Multiple Choice Questions (100 Questions per Paper).
- `[PAT-VAO-05]` **Marking Scheme:** +1.0 Mark for each correct answer.
- `[PAT-VAO-06]` **Negative Marking:** Deduction of 0.25 Mark (1/4th penalty) for every incorrect answer.
- `[PAT-VAO-07]` **Duration:** 2 Hours (120 Minutes) per Paper.
- `[PAT-VAO-08]` **Compulsory Qualifying Test:** Kannada Language Examination (150 Marks, 50 Marks qualifying threshold; qualifying only, not added to merit ranking).
- `[PAT-VAO-09]` **Question Paper Medium:** Bilingual (Kannada and English).

## II. Paper-I: General Knowledge & Rural Administration (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಜ್ಞಾನ ಮತ್ತು ಗ್ರಾಮೀಣ ಆಡಳಿತ) — 100 Marks / 100 Qs

### 1. History of India and Karnataka (ಭಾರತ ಮತ್ತು ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ ಇತಿಹಾಸ) — [18–20 Marks]
- `[P1-VAO-HIST-1.1]` Ancient and Medieval Karnataka: Kadambas, Badami Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Kalyana Chalukyas, Hoysalas, and Vijayanagara Empire.
- `[P1-VAO-HIST-1.2]` Bahmani and Adil Shahi kingdoms of the Deccan; Art, literature, and architectural monuments.
- `[P1-VAO-HIST-1.3]` Modern Mysore and Freedom Struggle: Hyder Ali and Tipu Sultan, Mysore Wodeyars, Sir M. Visvesvaraya, Kittur Rani Chennamma, Sangolli Rayanna (1831), 1857 revolt in Karnataka (Halagali Bedas), and Vidurashwatha firing (1938).
- `[P1-VAO-HIST-1.4]` Karnataka Unification Movement (ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ ಏಕೀಕರಣ ಚಳವಳಿ) and renaming of Mysore State on 1 November 1973.

### 2. Geography of Karnataka and India (ಭಾರತ ಮತ್ತು ಕರ್ನಾಟಕದ ಭೂಗೋಳಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ) — [16–18 Marks]
- `[P1-VAO-GEOG-2.1]` Physical geography of Karnataka: Coastal plain (Karavali), Malnad, and Bayaluseeme; highest peaks (Mullayanagiri).
- `[P1-VAO-GEOG-2.2]` River drainage systems of Karnataka: Krishna and its tributaries (Tungabhadra, Bhima, Malaprabha, Ghataprabha); Kaveri and its tributaries; West-flowing rivers (Sharavathi, Kali, Netravati).
- `[P1-VAO-GEOG-2.3]` Climate, soils (Red, Black/Regur, Laterite), agro-climatic zones, and major agricultural crops of Karnataka.
- `[P1-VAO-GEOG-2.4]` Physical divisions of India: Himalayas, Indo-Gangetic Plains, Peninsular Plateau, Western and Eastern Ghats, Coastal Plains, and Islands.

### 3. Indian Constitution and Polity (ಭಾರತೀಯ ಸಂವಿಧಾನ ಮತ್ತು ರಾಜಕೀಯ ವ್ಯವಸ್ಥೆ) — [16–18 Marks]
- `[P1-VAO-POL-3.1]` Preamble, Fundamental Rights (Articles 12–35), 5 Writs, and Fundamental Duties (Article 51A).
- `[P1-VAO-POL-3.2]` Directive Principles of State Policy (Part IV) and Constitutional Amendment procedures (Article 368).
- `[P1-VAO-POL-3.3]` Union and State Executive and Legislature: President, Prime Minister, Governor, Chief Minister, State Legislative Assembly and Council.
- `[P1-VAO-POL-3.4]` Judiciary (Supreme Court and High Courts), Election Commission of India, and Special Provisions for Karnataka under Article 371J.

### 4. Panchayat Raj Act, Rural Administration & Land Records (ಗ್ರಾಮ ಸ್ವರಾಜ್, ಪಂಚಾಯತ್ ರಾಜ್ ಮತ್ತು ಕಂದಾಯ ಆಡಳಿತ) — [18–20 Marks]
- `[P1-VAO-RDPR-4.1]` 73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992 and the Eleventh Schedule (29 functional subjects).
- `[P1-VAO-RDPR-4.2]` Karnataka Gram Swaraj and Panchayat Raj Act, 1993: Three-tier structure (Grama, Taluk, and Zilla Panchayats), powers, functions, finances, and 50% reservation for women.
- `[P1-VAO-RDPR-4.3]` State Revenue Administration Hierarchy: Deputy Commissioner (DC), Assistant Commissioner (AC), Tahsildar, Revenue Inspector (RI), and Village Administrative Officer (VAO).
- `[P1-VAO-RDPR-4.4]` Duties and responsibilities of the Village Administrative Officer: Maintenance of village accounts, crop inspection, birth and death reporting, disaster relief enumeration, and collection of land revenue.
- `[P1-VAO-RDPR-4.5]` Land Records Digitization and Statutory Framework: Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964; Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops (RTC / Pahani Form 16); Mutation process under Section 129; E-governance platforms (Bhoomi, Mojini v3, e-Swathu, Dishank).

### 5. Karnataka State Schemes, Budget & Current Affairs (ಪ್ರಚಲಿತ ವಿದ್ಯಮಾನಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಕಲ್ಯಾಣ ಯೋಜನೆಗಳು) — [16–18 Marks]
- `[P1-VAO-CA-5.1]` Five Flagship Guarantee Schemes of Karnataka: Gruha Lakshmi (₹2,000/mo DBT), Shakti (free bus travel), Yuva Nidhi (₹3,000/₹1,500 aid), Anna Bhagya (10 kg foodgrain / ₹34/kg DBT), and Gruha Jyothi (free electricity up to 200 units).
- `[P1-VAO-CA-5.2]` Karnataka State Budget 2026–27: Total outlay (₹4,48,004 crore), major sectoral allocations, rural infrastructure, and Mukhya Mantri Saura Krishi Yojane.
- `[P1-VAO-CA-5.3]` National and International Current Affairs: ISRO space missions, sports, summits, bilateral agreements, national awards, and books/authors.
- `[P1-VAO-CA-5.4]` Everyday Science, environment, and biodiversity: Human body, diseases, vitamins, nutrition, Western Ghats ecology, and climate change policies.

## III. Paper-II: Communication & Computer Literacy (ಸಂವಹನ ಮತ್ತು ಗಣಕಯಂತ್ರ ಸಾಕ್ಷರತೆ) — 100 Marks / 100 Qs

### Section 1: General Kannada (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಕನ್ನಡ) — 35 Marks (35 Questions)
- `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.1]` Kannada Alphabet & Phonology (ಕನ್ನಡ ವರ್ಣಮಾಲೆ): ಸ್ವರಗಳು, ವ್ಯಂಜನಗಳು (ಅಲ್ಪಪ್ರಾಣ, ಮಹಾಪ್ರಾಣ, ಅನುನಾಸಿಕ), ಯೋಗವಾಹಗಳು (ಅನುಸ್ವಾರ, ವಿಸರ್ಗ).
- `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.2]` Kannada Sandhi Rules (ಸಂಧಿ ನಿಯಮಗಳು):
  - ಕನ್ನಡ ಸಂಧಿಗಳು: ಲೋಪ ಸಂಧಿ, ಆಗಮ ಸಂಧಿ, ಆದೇಶ ಸಂಧಿ.
  - ಸಂಸ್ಕೃತ ಸಂಧಿಗಳು: ಸವರ್ಣದೀರ್ಘ ಸಂಧಿ, ಗುಣ ಸಂಧಿ, ವೃದ್ಧಿ ಸಂಧಿ, ಯಣ್ ಸಂಧಿ.
- `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.3]` Samasa (ಸಮಾಸಗಳು): ತತ್ಪುರುಷ ಸಮಾಸ, ಕರ್ಮಧಾರಯ ಸಮಾಸ, ದ್ವಿಗು ಸಮಾಸ, ಬಹುವ್ರೀಹಿ ಸಮಾಸ, ದ್ವಂದ್ವ ಸಮಾಸ, ಅಂಶಿ ಸಮಾಸ, ಕ್ರಿಯಾ ಸಮಾಸ, ಗಮಕ ಸಮಾಸ.
- `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.4]` Tatsama - Tadbhava (ತತ್ಸಮ - ತದ್ಭವ ಶಬ್ದಕೋಶ): ನಿಯಮಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಪರೀಕ್ಷಾ ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟ ಪದಗಳು.
- `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.5]` Vibhakti Pratyayagalu (ವಿಭಕ್ತಿ ಪ್ರತ್ಯಯಗಳು): ಪ್ರಥಮಾ ರಿಂದ ಸಪ್ತಮೀ ವರೆಗಿನ ಕಾರಕಾರ್ಥಗಳು.
- `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.6]` Idioms, Proverbs & Mnemonics (ನಾಣ್ಣುಡಿಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಗಾದೆ ಮಾತುಗಳು).
- `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.7]` Antonyms, Synonyms & Word Substitutes (ವಿರುದ್ಧಾರ್ಥಕ ಮತ್ತು ಸಮಾನಾರ್ಥಕ ಪದಗಳು).
- `[P2-VAO-KAN-1.8]` Sentence Correction & Error Spotting (ವಾಕ್ಯದೋಷ ತಿದ್ದುಪಡಿ ಮತ್ತು ಪ್ರಬಂಧ ಲಕ್ಷಣಗಳು).

### Section 2: General English (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಇಂಗ್ಲಿಷ್) — 35 Marks (35 Questions)
- `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.1]` Parts of Speech: Nouns, Pronouns, Adjectives, Verbs, Adverbs, Prepositions, Conjunctions, and Interjections.
- `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.2]` Tenses and Sequence of Tenses: Simple, Continuous, Perfect, and Perfect Continuous.
- `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.3]` Subject-Verb Agreement (Syntax Rules & Exceptions).
- `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.4]` Active and Passive Voice (Rules of Transformation across all tenses).
- `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.5]` Direct and Indirect Speech (Reporting speech transformations, tense shifts, pronoun changes).
- `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.6]` Vocabulary & Word Usage: Synonyms, Antonyms, Homonyms, One-Word Substitutions.
- `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.7]` Idioms and Phrasal Verbs (Commonly tested in KPSC/KEA exams).
- `[P2-VAO-ENG-2.8]` Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement, and Reading Comprehension Passages.

### Section 3: Computer Knowledge (ಗಣಕಯಂತ್ರ ಜ್ಞಾನ) — 30 Marks (30 Questions)
- `[P2-VAO-COMP-3.1]` Fundamentals of Computers: Computer generations, Hardware architecture (CPU, ALU, CU, Registers, Cache L1/L2/L3, RAM vs ROM, SSD vs HDD).
- `[P2-VAO-COMP-3.2]` Operating Systems: Windows, Linux (open-source, kernel), Android; File management and standard file extensions.
- `[P2-VAO-COMP-3.3]` Microsoft Office Suite:
  - MS Word: Formatting, shortcut keys, Mail Merge, tables.
  - MS Excel: Spreadsheets, basic formulas (`=SUM`, `=AVERAGE`, `=COUNT`, `=COUNTA`, `=IF`), absolute referencing (`$A$1`), charts.
  - MS PowerPoint: Slide creation, animations vs transitions, slide show shortcuts (F5, Shift+F5).
- `[P2-VAO-COMP-3.4]` Computer Networks and the Internet: LAN, WAN, IP addressing (IPv4 vs IPv6), DNS, Web Browsers, Search Engines.
- `[P2-VAO-COMP-3.5]` Email Protocols & Communication: SMTP, POP3, IMAP, port numbers, attachments.
- `[P2-VAO-COMP-3.6]` Cybersecurity & Karnataka E-Governance Portals: Malware, Phishing, Ransomware, Firewalls, Bhoomi, Kaveri 2.0, e-Swathu, Dishank, Kutumba, and e-Kshana.
