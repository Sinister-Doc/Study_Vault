> [!tip] Exam Focus
> Science gives 6–8 questions, almost all **everyday-science level**: units and instruments, acids/bases, metals/gases, human body basics, vitamins/deficiency diseases, and environment (pollution, Ozone, greenhouse gases). Skip derivations; memorise **fact → value/name** pairs.

## 1. Physics (ಭೌತಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ) — units and everyday facts

| Quantity | SI unit | Instrument / note |
|---|---|---|
| Length | metre (ಮೀಟರ್) | Scale, vernier |
| Mass | kilogram | Balance |
| Force / Weight | newton (ನ್ಯೂಟನ್) | Spring balance |
| Pressure | pascal | Barometer (atmospheric) |
| Energy / Work | joule | — |
| Power | watt | — |
| Electric current | ampere | Ammeter |
| Temperature | kelvin | Thermometer; 0°C = 273.15 K |
| Frequency | hertz | — |

Everyday facts: light travels ~3×10⁸ m/s (sound ~343 m/s — hence lightning before thunder); rainbow = dispersion; fuse wire = low melting point alloy; MCB trips on overload.

## 2. Chemistry (ರಸಾಯನಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ)

- **Acids–bases–salts (ಆಮ್ಲ–ಪ್ರತ್ಯಾಮ್ಲ):** pH < 7 acid, 7 neutral, >7 base; litmus: blue→red in acid, red→blue in base. Common pairs: HCl (hydrochloric), H₂SO₄ (sulphuric), NaOH (caustic soda), NaHCO₃ (baking soda), CaOCl₂ (bleaching powder — water disinfectant, VAO-relevant for village water supply).
- **Metals:** most reactive K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > Cu > Ag > Au (learn order: "**ಪೊ–ಸೋ–ಕ್ಯಾ–ಮ್ಯಾ–ಅ–ಜಿ–ಐ–ಲೆ–ಕಾ–ಬೆ–ಚಿ**" by first letters); iron rusts (needs air + moisture; galvanisation with zinc prevents it); aluminium most abundant metal in crust.
- **Gases:** O₂ supports combustion, CO₂ used in fire extinguishers and photosynthesis input; LPG = propane+butane (odour from ethyl mercaptan); CNG = methane; photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂.

## 3. Biology (ಜೀವಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ)

- **Human body:** 206 bones (adult), largest bone femur; normal body temp 37°C/98.6°F; blood groups ABO + Rh (universal donor O−, universal recipient AB+); haemoglobin carries O₂; insulin (pancreas) controls blood sugar — deficiency → diabetes.
- **Vitamins (ಜೀವಸತ್ವಗಳು):**

| Vitamin | Source | Deficiency |
|---|---|---|
| A | Carrot, mango, green leaves | Night blindness (ರಾತ್ರಿ ಕುರುಡು) |
| B-complex (B1/B12) | Whole grains, milk, egg | Beriberi / anaemia |
| C | Citrus, amla, guava | Scurvy |
| D | Sunlight, milk, fish | Rickets |
| E | Vegetable oils, nuts | (Antioxidant; rare deficiency) |
| K | Green leaves, gut bacteria | Excess bleeding |

- **Plants:** photosynthesis in chlorophyll; xylem carries water up, phloem carries food; nitrogen-fixing bacteria (Rhizobium) in legume roots — why crop rotation with pulses enriches soil (links to [[05_Economy]] agriculture).

## 4. Environment (ಪರಿಸರ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ)

- Greenhouse gases: CO₂, CH₄, N₂O (mnemonic "**ಕಾರ್ಬನ್–ಮೀಥೇನ್–ನೈಟ್ರಸ್**"); Ozone layer (O₃) blocks UV — depleted by CFCs; Montreal Protocol protects it.
- Pollution control: BOD (biological oxygen demand — high BOD = polluted water); EIA before projects; 3Rs — Reduce, Reuse, Recycle.
- Karnataka touch: Western Ghats = biodiversity hotspot (UNESCO); Bandipur–Nagarahole–BRT tiger belt; Jog/Kaveri basins for hydel vs ecology questions.

## Likely Questions / PYQ

1. SI unit of force? — (a) Newton ✔ (b) Joule (c) Watt (d) Pascal
2. pH of neutral solution? — (a) 7 ✔ (b) 0 (c) 14 (d) 1
3. Baking soda formula? — (a) NaHCO₃ ✔ (b) Na₂CO₃ (c) NaOH (d) CaCO₃
4. Universal blood donor group? — (a) O negative ✔ (b) AB positive (c) A positive (d) B negative
5. Vitamin D deficiency causes? — (a) Rickets ✔ (b) Scurvy (c) Night blindness (d) Goitre
6. Gas filled in LPG cylinders (main)? — (a) Propane + butane ✔ (b) Methane only (c) Oxygen (d) Hydrogen
7. Ozone layer depletion is caused by? — (a) CFCs ✔ (b) CO₂ only (c) SO₂ (d) N₂
