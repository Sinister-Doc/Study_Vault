> [!tip] Exam Focus
> Economy gives 5–7 questions: one each on GDP/inflation basics, banking, agriculture, MGNREGA, and 1–2 Karnataka schemes the VAO actually implements (Anna Bhagya, Gruha Lakshmi, crop insurance, pension schemes). Learn **scheme → benefit → beneficiary** as a triple; the paper tests the middle link.

## 1. Basic Economics (ಮೂಲ ಅರ್ಥಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ)

- **GDP (ಒಟ್ಟು ದೇಶೀಯ ಉತ್ಪನ್ನ):** value of final goods/services in a year; India ~5th largest nominal economy; three sectors — primary (agriculture), secondary (industry), tertiary (services, largest share).
- **Inflation (ಹಣದುಬ್ಬರ):** CPI (retail, RBI targets 4% ± 2%) vs WPI (wholesale); asked as "which index measures retail inflation" → CPI.
- **Banking:** RBI (ಭಾರತೀಯ ರಿಸರ್ವ್ ಬ್ಯಾಂಕ್, 1935; banker's bank, note issuer, repo rate setter) → commercial banks → RRBs/co-operative banks serving villages; NABARD refinances rural credit; SHGs (ಸ್ವಸಹಾಯ ಗುಂಪು) and Stree Shakti groups channel micro-credit.

## 2. Indian Agriculture (ಭಾರತೀಯ ಕೃಷಿ)

- ~45% of workforce, ~18% of GDP; land-use: net sown area, cropping intensity; MSP (ಕನಿಷ್ಠ ಬೆಂಬಲ ಬೆಲೆ) by CACP for 23 crops; Green Revolution (ಹಸಿರು ಕ್ರಾಂತಿ — wheat/rice, Punjab/Haryana, M.S. Swaminathan).
- Karnataka crops: paddy (Mandya/Raichur command areas), ragi (finger millet — southern Maidan staple), jowar (north), sugarcane (Belagavi/Mandya belt), cotton (black-soil north), coffee/areca (Malnad) — see [[03_Geography]].

## 3. Rural Development and Welfare Schemes (ಗ್ರಾಮೀಣಾಭಿವೃದ್ಧಿ ಯೋಜನೆಗಳು)

| Scheme | Benefit (the tested link) | Beneficiary |
|---|---|---|
| MGNREGA (ಮಹಾತ್ಮ ಗಾಂಧಿ ಉದ್ಯೋಗ ಖಾತರಿ) | 100 days wage employment/household/year | Rural households |
| PMAY-G (ಗ್ರಾಮೀಣ ವಸತಿ) | Assistance for pucca house | Houseless rural poor |
| PM-KISAN | ₹6,000/year in 3 instalments | Land-holding farmer families |
| PM Fasal Bima (ಬೆಳೆ ವಿಮೆ) | Crop-loss insurance (low farmer premium) | Farmers (VAO reports crop loss) |
| Ayushman Bharat–PMJAY (+ state Arogya Karnataka) | ₹5 lakh health cover/family/year | Poor/vulnerable families |
| Anna Bhagya (ಅನ್ನ ಭಾಗ್ಯ, Karnataka) | Free rice + DBT in lieu of extra grain | BPL families |
| Gruha Lakshmi (ಗೃಹ ಲಕ್ಷ್ಮಿ, Karnataka) | ₹2,000/month to woman head | BPL/APL women heads |
| Old-age/widow/disability pensions (ವೃದ್ಧಾಪ್ಯ/ವಿಧವಾ/ವಿಕಲಚೇತನ) | Monthly pension via DBT | Eligible elderly/widows/disabled |
| Swachh Bharat-G / Jal Jeevan Mission | Toilets / tap water per household | Rural households |

**VAO link (frequently asked indirectly):** the VAO's RTC, income/caste certificates, crop-cutting and loss reports are the **ground-truth inputs** that decide eligibility and payouts for PM-KISAN, Fasal Bima, pensions, and housing lists. Any "who verifies beneficiary records at village level" question → VAO.

**Mnemonics:** sectors "**ಪ್ರಾ–ದ್ವಿ–ತೃ**" (primary–secondary–tertiary; tertiary largest). Central trio "**ಉದ್ಯೋಗ–ವಸತಿ–ಆರೋಗ್ಯ**" (MGNREGA–PMAY–PMJAY). Karnataka pair "**ಅನ್ನ–ಗೃಹ**" (Anna Bhagya = food, Gruha Lakshmi = cash to woman head).

## Likely Questions / PYQ

1. MGNREGA guarantees how many days of employment? — (a) 100 ✔ (b) 120 (c) 150 (d) 200
2. PM-KISAN annual benefit? — (a) ₹6,000 ✔ (b) ₹5,000 (c) ₹12,000 (d) ₹2,000
3. Retail inflation in India is measured by? — (a) CPI ✔ (b) WPI (c) IIP (d) GDP deflator only
4. PM Fasal Bima Yojana is a scheme for? — (a) Crop insurance ✔ (b) Irrigation subsidy (c) Soil testing (d) Farm machinery
5. Gruha Lakshmi gives ₹2,000/month to? — (a) Woman head of family ✔ (b) Every adult (c) Farmers only (d) Senior citizens
6. RBI was established in? — (a) 1935 ✔ (b) 1947 (c) 1949 (d) 1951

## Quick Revision

> [!abstract] 60-second revision
> - GDP 3 sectors (tertiary biggest); CPI = retail inflation (RBI 4±2%); RBI 1935, NABARD = rural credit
> - MSP by CACP (23 crops); Green Revolution = wheat/rice
> - Triples: MGNREGA–100 days; PM-KISAN–₹6k; PMJAY–₹5 lakh; Anna Bhagya–rice; Gruha Lakshmi–₹2k to woman head
> - VAO = ground-truth (RTC, crop-loss reports) behind every rural payout
