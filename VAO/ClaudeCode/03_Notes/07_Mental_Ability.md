> [!tip] Exam Focus
> Mental ability is 10–15 pure-scoring questions: number series, coding-decoding, blood relations, direction sense, odd-one-out, syllogisms, percentages/averages, and simple/time-work arithmetic. No topic needs theory beyond the one-line rule below — **speed comes from recognising the pattern type in 10 seconds**.

## 1. Number Series (ಸಂಖ್ಯಾ ಶ್ರೇಣಿ)

Check in order: (a) constant difference (+n), (b) growing difference (+2, +4, +6…), (c) multiply (×2, ×3), (d) squares/cubes (1, 4, 9, 16… / 1, 8, 27, 64…), (e) alternate two series interleaved, (f) digit operations. Worked: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ? → differences 4, 6, 8, 10 → next +12 = **42**.

## 2. Coding-Decoding (ಸಂಕೇತ)

- Letter-shift: A→C, B→D = +2 each (CAT → ECV). Test by first letter, apply to all.
- Reverse alphabet: A↔Z, B↔Y (A=1, Z=26; pair sums to 27). "BAD" → YZW.
- Number code: A=1…Z=26 (CAT = 3+1+20 = 24).

## 3. Blood Relations (ರಕ್ತ ಸಂಬಂಧ)

Draw one line per relation, mark gender. Key Kannada terms: ತಂದೆ/ತಾಯಿ (father/mother), ಮಗ/ಮಗಳು (son/daughter), ಅಣ್ಣ/ತಮ್ಮ (elder/younger brother), ಅಕ್ಕ/ತಂಗಿ (elder/younger sister), ಮಾವ/ಅತ್ತೆ (uncle-in-law/aunt or father/mother-in-law), ಅಳಿಯ/ಸೊಸೆ (son/daughter-in-law), ಮೊಮ್ಮಗ/ಮೊಮ್ಮಗಳು (grandson/granddaughter). Worked: "A is B's brother. B is C's mother. A is C's ___?" → A and B are siblings, B is mother → A is **maternal uncle (ಸೋದರಮಾವ)**.

## 4. Direction Sense (ದಿಕ್ಕು)

Face north: right = east, left = west. Standard map order clockwise N–E–S–W (mnemonic "**ಉ–ಪೂ–ದ–ಪ**": ಉತ್ತರ–ಪೂರ್ವ–ದಕ್ಷಿಣ–ಪಶ್ಚಿಮ). Worked: walk 3 km north, turn right (east) 4 km → distance from start = 3-4-5 triangle = **5 km**.

## 5. Syllogisms (ನ್ಯಾಯವಾಕ್ಯ)

Only three valid moves most VAO items need: (1) All A are B + All B are C → All A are C; (2) All A are B + No B is C → No A is C; (3) Some A are B + All B are C → Some A are C. "Some + Some" gives **no conclusion** — the commonest trap.

## 6. Basic Arithmetic (ಮೂಲ ಗಣಿತ)

- Percentage: x% of N = xN/100. Average = sum/count.
- Ratio-proportion: divide in ratio a:b → shares a/(a+b), b/(a+b).
- Time–work: 1-day work adds; A in 10 days + B in 15 → together 1/10+1/15 = 1/6 → **6 days**.
- Simple interest: I = PRT/100.

## 10 Practice Questions (with answers)

1. Series: 3, 9, 27, 81, ? — (a) 243 ✔ (×3)
2. Series: 5, 11, 23, 47, ? — (a) 95 ✔ (×2+1)
3. If DOG = 26 (4+15+7), CAT = ? — (a) 24 ✔ (3+1+20)
4. A→D (+3): if BALL codes DDOO, CALL codes? — (a) FDOO ✔
5. Pointing to a photo, Ravi says "She is my mother's only daughter." Who is she? — (a) Ravi's sister (his sibling if male; himself if female — here, sister) ✔
6. Facing east, you turn left, then left again. Now facing? — (a) West ✔ (E→N→W)
7. Statements: All pens are books; All books are tables. Conclusion? — (a) All pens are tables ✔
8. Statements: Some flowers are red; Some red are roses. Conclusion? — (a) No definite conclusion ✔
9. Average of 12, 18, 24, 30? — (a) 21 ✔ (84/4)
10. A completes work in 12 days, B in 6 days. Together? — (a) 4 days ✔ (1/12+1/6 = 1/4)

## Quick Revision

> [!abstract] 60-second revision
> - Series order: difference → growing difference → multiply → squares/cubes → alternate
> - Codes: check shift first, then A↔Z (sum 27), then A=1 numbering
> - Relations: draw tree, mark gender; maternal vs paternal uncle split
> - Directions: N–E–S–W clockwise; 3-4-5 triangle shortcut
> - Syllogism: Some+Some = no conclusion; arithmetic: SI = PRT/100, combined work adds reciprocals
