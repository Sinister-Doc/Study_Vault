## 1. Grammar (ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ)

### Parts of Speech (ವ್ಯಾಕರಣಿಗಳ ಪಕ್ರಮ)

| Part | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Noun** | Name of person/place/thing | Teacher, Bangalore, Book |
| **Pronoun** | Replaces a noun | He, She, It, They |
| **Adjective** | Describes a noun | Good, Happy, Tall |
| **Verb** | Shows action/state | Run, Sleep, Is |
| **Adverb** | Describes a verb/adjective | Quickly, Very, Well |
| **Preposition** | Shows relationship | In, On, At, By |
| **Conjunction** | Joins words/sentences | And, But, Because |
| **Interjection** | Shows emotion | Wow!, Ouch!, Hurray! |

> [!example] Likely Questions
> Q: Identify the part of speech: "Quickly" — Adverb
> Q: Identify the part of speech: "The" — Article (Determiner)

### Tenses (ಕಾಲ)

#### Present Tense (ವರ್ತಮಾನ ಕಾಲ)

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple Present** | Subject + V1 | He plays cricket |
| **Present Continuous** | Subject + am/is/are + V-ing | He is playing cricket |
| **Present Perfect** | Subject + have/has + V3 | He has played cricket |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Subject + have/has been + V-ing | He has been playing |

#### Past Tense (ಭೂತಕಾಲ)

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple Past** | Subject + V2 | He played cricket |
| **Past Continuous** | Subject + was/were + V-ing | He was playing cricket |
| **Past Perfect** | Subject + had + V3 | He had played cricket |
| **Past Perfect Continuous** | Subject + had been + V-ing | He had been playing |

#### Future Tense (ಭವಿಷ್ಯತ್ ಕಾಲ)

| Form | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple Future** | Subject + shall/will + V1 | He will play cricket |
| **Future Continuous** | Subject + will be + V-ing | He will be playing |
| **Future Perfect** | Subject + will have + V3 | He will have played |
| **Future Perfect Continuous** | Subject + will have been + V-ing | He will have been playing |

### Subject-Verb Agreement (ವಿಷಯ-ಕ್ರಿಯ ಸಮಾನತೆ)

- Singular subject → singular verb
- Plural subject → plural verb
- Examples:
  - A boy **is** playing. (Singular)
  - The boys **are** playing. (Plural)
  - Each of the students **is** present. (Indefinite pronominal adjective → singular)

> [!example] Common Errors
> ❌ Each of the candidates are present → ✅ Each of the candidates is present
> ❌ The team are playing → ✅ The team is playing (team = collective noun, singular in AmE)

### Direct & Indirect Speech (෨කණ හා අප්‍රිකතිත කතාව)

**Direct**: He said, "I am busy."
**Indirect**: He said that he was busy.

### Active & Passive Voice (ಸಕ್ರಿಯ ಕತಾವಲಿ ಹಾಗೂ ನಿಷ್ಕ್ರಿಯ ಕತಾವಲಿ)

| Active | Passive |
|---|---|
| He kicks the ball | The ball is kicked by him |
| She writes a letter | A letter is written by her |

### Sentence Rearrangement (ವಾಕ್ಯ ಪುನರ್ವಿನ್ಯಾಸ)

Given jumbled sentences, arrange them into a logical sequence:
- Look for the introductory sentence (often starts with "Once upon a time" or sets context)
- Identify connecting words: First, Next, Then, Finally, Therefore, However
- Ensure logical flow: Introduction → Supporting → Conclusion

## 3. Error Spotting (ದೋಷ ಕಂಡೆತ)

**Instruction**: In each sentence, one part contains an error. Identify it.

> [!example]
> ❌ "Each of the students are preparing well." → "are" should be "is" (subject-verb disagreement)
> ❌ "She gave him a piece of advices." → "advices" should be "advice" (uncountable noun)
> ❌ "He is superior than me." → "than me" should be "to me" (superior to)

Common error types:
1. **Subject-verb disagreement** (e.g., "Neither he nor I am" → "am" should be "are")
2. **Tense inconsistency** (mixing past and present without reason)
3. **Preposition errors** (e.g., "discuss about" → "discuss")
4. **Article errors** (a, an, the usage)
5. **Spelling mistakes**

## 5. Comprehension (ಅರ್ಥಮಾಡಿಕೆ)

**Strategy**:
1. Read the passage quickly first (overall idea)
2. Read questions
3. Go back to relevant lines in the passage
4. Answer based on what is stated/implied

**Types of questions**:
- **Literal**: Directly stated in the passage
- **Inferential**: Requires inference/logic
- **Vocabulary-based**: Meaning of words in context
- **Theme-based**: Title/main idea

> [!example] Common trap: Inference questions that ask "The author would agree that..." — you must infer from the passage, not your own opinion.

### Reading Comprehension Tips
- Look for topic sentences (first line of paragraph usually)
- Underline or note key points while reading
- Watch for qualifying words: "but", "however", "although" (they reverse meaning)
- Practice active reading — ask yourself: What is the author trying to say?

## 6. Letter Writing (ಪತ್ರ ಬರಹ)

### Formal Letter Format (ಔಪಚಾರಿಕ ಪತ್ರ)

```
Sender's Address
City, State — PIN Code
Date

Recipient's Name
Designation
Organization
Address

Subject: [Subject line if applicable]

Sir/Madam,

[Body of the letter — introduction, details, closing]

Yours faithfully,
[Your Name]
[Your Designation, if any]
```

**Types**: Complaint letter, application, request letter, order letter

### Informal Letter Format (ಅನೌಪಚಾರಿಕ ಪತ್ರ)

```
Your address (bottom left or top right)
Date

Dear [Name],

Body paragraphs — casual tone, personal feelings

Love/affectionately,
[Your Name]
```
