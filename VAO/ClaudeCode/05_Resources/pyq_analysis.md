## Most-expected questions (all [expected], NOT PYQs)

### Kannada grammar (Q1–15)

**Q1 [expected].** ಮನೆ + ಅಲ್ಲಿ = ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲಿ — ಸಂಧಿ ಯಾವುದು? A) ಲೋಪ ಸಂಧಿ B) ಆಗಮ ಸಂಧಿ (ಯಕಾರಾಗಮ) C) ಆದೇಶ ಸಂಧಿ D) ಗುಣ ಸಂಧಿ
- Basis: sandhi identification is the highest-frequency Kannada grammar item.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: ಯ್ is inserted between the words, i.e. ಆಗಮ.

**Q2 [expected].** ವಿದ್ಯೆ + ಆಲಯ = ವಿದ್ಯಾಲಯ — ಸಂಧಿ? A) ಗುಣ B) ವೃದ್ಧಿ C) ಸವರ್ಣದೀರ್ಘ D) ಯಣ್
- Basis: textbook savarna example, repeatedly asked.
- Answer: **C**. Explanation: ಅ + ಆ = ಆ (same-vowel lengthening).

**Q3 [expected].** ಸೂರ್ಯ + ಉದಯ = ಸೂರ್ಯೋದಯ — ಸಂಧಿ? A) ಸವರ್ಣದೀರ್ಘ B) ಗುಣ C) ವೃದ್ಧಿ D) ಜಶ್ತ್ವ
- Basis: guna-sandhi classic example.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: ಅ + ಉ = ಓ, the ಗುಣ outcome.

**Q4 [expected].** "ಅನ್ನದಾನ" — ಸಮಾಸ? A) ದ್ವಂದ್ವ B) ಷಷ್ಠಿ ತತ್ಪುರುಷ C) ಬಹುವ್ರೀಹಿ D) ಅವ್ಯಯೀಭಾವ
- Basis: tatpurusha with elided case suffix dominates samasa items.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: expands to "ಅನ್ನದ ದಾನ" (6th-case sense).

**Q5 [expected].** "ತಾಯ್ತಂದೆ" — ಸಮಾಸ? A) ದ್ವಿಗು B) ದ್ವಂದ್ವ C) ತತ್ಪುರುಷ D) ಕರ್ಮಧಾರಯ
- Basis: paired-word dvandva items repeat.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: ತಾಯಿ + ತಂದೆ, both principal, joined by 'and'.

**Q6 [expected].** "ಚತುರ್ಭುಜ" (ವಿಷ್ಣುವಿನ ಅರ್ಥದಲ್ಲಿ) — ಸಮಾಸ? A) ದ್ವಿಗು B) ದ್ವಂದ್ವ C) ಬಹುವ್ರೀಹಿ D) ತತ್ಪುರುಷ
- Basis: bahuvrihi third-meaning test is the standard trap.
- Answer: **C**. Explanation: denotes the possessor (one with four arms), not the arms.

**Q7 [expected].** "ಅವನಿಂದ" — ವಿಭಕ್ತಿ? A) ಪ್ರಥಮಾ B) ದ್ವಿತೀಯಾ C) ತೃತೀಯಾ D) ಚತುರ್ಥಿ
- Basis: vibhakti-by-suffix is a staple.
- Answer: **C**. Explanation: -ಇಂದ marks ಕರಣಾರ್ಥಕ ತೃತೀಯಾ.

**Q8 [expected].** "ಅವನಿಗೆ ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಕೊಟ್ಟೆ" — 'ಗೆ' ವಿಭಕ್ತಿ? A) ತೃತೀಯಾ B) ಚತುರ್ಥಿ C) ಪಂಚಮಿ D) ಸಪ್ತಮಿ
- Basis: -ಗೆ/-ಕ್ಕೆ = chaturthi is asked almost every cycle.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: recipient (ಸಂಪ್ರದಾನ) takes ಚತುರ್ಥಿ.

**Q9 [expected].** 'ಸೂರ್ಯ'ನ ಸಮಾನಾರ್ಥಕ ಪದ? A) ಚಂದ್ರ B) ಆದಿತ್ಯ C) ಅಗ್ನಿ D) ವಾಯು
- Basis: sun-synonym is a staple vocabulary item.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: ಆದಿತ್ಯ = ಸೂರ್ಯ; others are moon, fire, wind.

**Q10 [expected].** 'ಕಮಲ'ದ ಸಮಾನಾರ್ಥಕ? A) ಪಂಕಜ B) ಪರ್ವತ C) ನದಿ D) ಮರ
- Basis: lotus-synonym repeats in vocab sets.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: ಪಂಕಜ (mud-born) = ಕಮಲ.

**Q11 [expected].** 'ಸುಖ' ವಿರುದ್ಧಾರ್ಥಕ? A) ಸಂತೋಷ B) ಆನಂದ C) ದುಃಖ D) ನೆಮ್ಮದಿ
- Basis: antonym pairs are staple marks.
- Answer: **C**. Explanation: ಸುಖ × ದುಃಖ is the standard pair; A/B/D are synonyms.

**Q12 [expected].** 'ಜಯ' ವಿರುದ್ಧಾರ್ಥಕ? A) ವಿಜಯ B) ಪರಾಜಯ C) ಗೆಲುವು D) ಯಶಸ್ಸು
- Basis: jaya-pair antonym repeats.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: ಜಯ × ಪರಾಜಯ; A/C/D all mean victory.

**Q13 [expected].** "ಕೈ ಚೆಲ್ಲಿ ಕುಳಿತುಕೊಳ್ಳುವುದು" — ಅರ್ಥ? A) ಸಹಾಯ ಮಾಡುವುದು B) ಪ್ರಯತ್ನ ಕೈಬಿಡುವುದು C) ಕೆಲಸ ಶುರುಮಾಡುವುದು D) ಹಣ ಉಳಿಸುವುದು
- Basis: idiom-meaning items repeat.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: the idiom means giving up effort.

**Q14 [expected].** 'ಕ್ಷೀರ'ದ ತದ್ಭವ ರೂಪ? A) ಹಾಲು B) ನೀರು C) ಬೆಲ್ಲ D) ಅಕ್ಕಿ
- Basis: tatsama–tadbhav conversion is a staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: Sanskrit ಕ್ಷೀರ → Kannada ಹಾಲು.

**Q15 [expected].** ಶುದ್ಧ ರೂಪ ಯಾವುದು? A) ವಿದ್ಯಾಲಯ B) ವಿದ್ಯಾಲೈ C) ವಿಧ್ಯಾಲಯ D) ವಿದ್ಯಾಲಯಾ
- Basis: correct-spelling items repeat.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: only A matches standard orthography.

### History incl. Karnataka dynasties (Q16–21)

**Q16 [expected].** Vijayanagara Empire founded (1336) by? A) Harihara I & Bukka Raya B) Krishnadevaraya C) Tipu Sultan D) Pulakeshin II
- Basis: single most-asked Karnataka history fact.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: Sangama brothers founded it; Krishnadevaraya ruled later (1509–29).

**Q17 [expected].** Hoysala capital? A) Badami B) Dwarasamudra (Halebidu) C) Manyakheta D) Kalyani
- Basis: dynasty–capital mapping staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: Dwarasamudra = Halebidu; Badami = Chalukya, Manyakheta = Rashtrakuta.

**Q18 [expected].** Aihole inscription of Pulakeshin II composed by? A) Ravikirti B) Kalidasa C) Banabhatta D) Amoghavarsha I
- Basis: inscription–poet linkage staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: court poet Ravikirti authored the Aihole prashasti.

**Q19 [expected].** Kavirajamarga (earliest Kannada literary work) author? A) Pampa B) Amoghavarsha I C) Ranna D) Ponna
- Basis: first-Kannada-text question staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: Rashtrakuta king Amoghavarsha I; Pampa's Vikramarjuna Vijaya came later.

**Q20 [expected].** Tipu Sultan died in which war/year/place? A) 1799, Fourth Anglo-Mysore War, Srirangapatna B) 1782, Arcot C) 1792, Bengaluru D) 1806, Vellore
- Basis: Tipu-death fact staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: killed May 1799 defending Srirangapatna.

**Q21 [expected].** Karnataka unification (Vishal Mysore State)? A) 1 Nov 1956 B) 15 Aug 1947 C) 26 Jan 1950 D) 1 Nov 1973
- Basis: state-formation date staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: States Reorganisation Act; 1973 only renamed Mysore → Karnataka.

### Geography — Karnataka districts/rivers (Q22–26)

**Q22 [expected].** Largest Karnataka district by area? A) Belagavi B) Bengaluru Urban C) Kodagu D) Dakshina Kannada
- Basis: district-area staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: Belagavi (~13,415 sq km) is the largest.

**Q23 [expected].** River Kaveri origin? A) Talakaveri (Kodagu) B) Mullayanagiri C) Nandi Hills D) Kudremukh
- Basis: river-origin staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: rises at Talakaveri, Brahmagiri hills, Kodagu.

**Q24 [expected].** Jog Falls — river and district? A) Sharavathi–Shivamogga B) Kaveri–Mandya C) Krishna–Raichur D) Tunga–Udupi
- Basis: falls–river mapping staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: Sharavathi plunges at Jog near Sagara, Shivamogga.

**Q25 [expected].** Black (regur) soil belt known for which crop/region? A) Cotton, North Karnataka plateau B) Tea, Kodagu C) Apple, plains D) Coconut, coast only
- Basis: soil–crop mapping staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: moisture-retentive Deccan black soil suits cotton.

**Q26 [expected].** Highest peak in Karnataka? A) Mullayanagiri B) Tadiandamol C) Kudremukh D) Nandi Hills
- Basis: state-highest-peak staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: Mullayanagiri (1,930 m), Chikkamagaluru; others are lower.

### Polity — Constitution & Panchayati Raj, heavy (Q27–34)

**Q27 [expected].** 73rd Constitutional Amendment? A) 1992 (in force 1993), added Part IX B) 1976 C) 1985 D) 2002
- Basis: PRI amendment-number staple; VAO-heavy topic.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: added Part IX + 11th Schedule (29 subjects).

**Q28 [expected].** Village panchayats directed under which DPSP Article? A) Art 32 B) Art 40 C) Art 370 D) Art 280
- Basis: DPSP–Panchayat link staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: Art 40 directs the state to organise village panchayats.

**Q29 [expected].** Karnataka Panchayat Raj Act year? A) 1983 B) 1993 C) 2005 D) 2010
- Basis: state PRI Act staple for VAO.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: 1993 Act aligns the state with the 73rd Amendment.

**Q30 [expected].** Grama Sabha comprises? A) All registered voters of the village B) Only elected members C) Only officials D) Only women members
- Basis: Grama Sabha composition staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: Art 243(b): all persons on the electoral roll.

**Q31 [expected].** Women reservation in Karnataka PRIs? A) 33% B) 50% C) 10% D) None
- Basis: reservation-percentage staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: Karnataka raised PRI reservation to 50% (73rd mandates minimum one-third).

**Q32 [expected].** PRI elections conducted by? A) State Election Commission (Art 243K) B) ECI directly C) Panchayat secretary D) District Collector alone
- Basis: election-authority staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: Art 243K vests PRI elections in the SEC.

**Q33 [expected].** Panchayat term? A) 3 years B) 5 years C) 6 years D) Lifetime
- Basis: PRI term staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: Art 243E: five years unless sooner dissolved.

**Q34 [expected].** 74th Amendment concerns? A) Municipalities/urban bodies + 12th Schedule B) Panchayats C) SC/ST commissions D) Official languages
- Basis: 73rd-vs-74th contrast staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: added Part IX-A + 12th Schedule (18 functions).

### General Science (Q39–42)

**Q39 [expected].** Photosynthesis occurs in? A) Mitochondria B) Chlorophyll/chloroplast C) Nucleus D) Ribosome
- Basis: core bio-process staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: chlorophyll traps light energy in chloroplasts.

**Q40 [expected].** Scurvy is caused by deficiency of? A) Vitamin C B) Vitamin D C) Iron D) Protein
- Basis: deficiency–disease staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: vitamin C (ascorbic acid); D deficiency causes rickets.

**Q41 [expected].** pH of pure water at 25°C? A) 0 B) 1 C) 7 D) 14
- Basis: chemistry-constant staple.
- Answer: **C**. Explanation: 7 is neutral on the 0–14 scale.

**Q42 [expected].** Universal RBC donor group? A) AB+ B) O− C) B+ D) A−
- Basis: blood-group staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: O− red cells lack A, B and Rh antigens.

### Mental Ability (Q52–60)

**Q52 [expected].** Series: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ? A) 40 B) 42 C) 44 D) 36
- Basis: n(n+1) difference-series staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: gaps 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 → 30 + 12 = 42.

**Q53 [expected].** Average of 12, 18, 24, 30, 36? A) 22 B) 24 C) 26 D) 28
- Basis: basic-average staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: sum 120 ÷ 5 = 24.

**Q54 [expected].** 15% of 240? A) 30 B) 36 C) 40 D) 45
- Basis: percentage-computation staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: 240 × 0.15 = 36.

**Q55 [expected].** A:B = 2:3, sum 50 → A? A) 30 B) 20 C) 25 D) 15
- Basis: ratio-share staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: 2/5 × 50 = 20.

**Q56 [expected].** If CAT = 3+1+20 = 24, DOG = ? A) 26 B) 24 C) 28 D) 32
- Basis: letter-sum coding staple.
- Answer: **A**. Explanation: 4 + 15 + 7 = 26.

**Q57 [expected].** Odd one out: A) Mango B) Banana C) Potato D) Apple
- Basis: classification staple.
- Answer: **C**. Explanation: potato is a root vegetable; the rest are fruits.

**Q58 [expected].** Simple interest on Rs 10,000 at 8% for 2 years? A) Rs 800 B) Rs 1,600 C) Rs 2,000 D) Rs 1,800
- Basis: simple-interest staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: 10,000 × 0.08 × 2 = 1,600.

**Q59 [expected].** Facing north, turn right, then right again — now facing? A) North B) South C) East D) West
- Basis: direction-turn staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: two right turns = 180°, north → south.

**Q60 [expected].** 5 workers make 5 units in 5 days → 10 workers in 10 days make? A) 10 B) 20 C) 25 D) 50
- Basis: work-rate staple.
- Answer: **B**. Explanation: 0.2 unit/worker-day × 100 worker-days = 20.
