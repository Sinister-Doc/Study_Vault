> [!tip] How to use the PYQ archive in the days you have left
> Do not try to read all of it. Download **VAO 2024 Paper 1, VAO 2024 Paper 2**, and **one GK paper
> plus one KEC paper** from 2025. Time yourself on Paper 1 and Paper 2 under the real 120-minute
> rule. Then skim the two extra papers only for topics you have not seen before. That is roughly 400
> real questions — far more valuable than any third-party question bank.

## 5. Gaps — sought, not found, not invented

1. **A VAO 2026 or 2025 question paper does not exist yet** — the exam is on 04.10.2026. The 2024
   papers in §3.1 are the most recent real ones.
2. **Karnataka Land Revenue Act 1964 official PDF** — India Code returned HTTP 403 and the DPAL link
   failed TLS verification in an earlier pass, so no verified Act PDF is listed. Use the India Code
   portal manually if you need the bare Act.
3. **A text-extractable copy of any KEA paper** — all KEA papers are scanned images, so they cannot be
   searched or copied. This is why the mocks in `../06_Mock_Tests/` do not claim to reproduce them.
4. **An official KEA answer key for the VAO 2024 papers** — KEA publishes answer keys during the
   objection window; the key PDFs for 2024 were not located. So the mock answer keys in this kit are
   our own, not official. See `../06_Mock_Tests/mock_test_notes.md` §4.
