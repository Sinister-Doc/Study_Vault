## Paper 2 — Specific Paper (ನಿರ್ದಿಷ್ಟ ಪತ್ರಿಕೆ): 100 Q / 100 marks

| Section | Subject | Question Count (verified) |
|---|---|---|
| (ಎ) | General Kannada (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಕನ್ನಡ) | 25–30 questions |

### GK Sub-topics (each gets its own note section)

- **History (ಇತಿಹಾಸ)**: Ancient India, Medieval India, Modern India, Karnataka History (Chalukyas, Rashtrakutas, Hoysalas, Vijayanagara, Wodeyars, freedom movement in Karnataka)
- **Geography (ಭೂಗೋಳ)**: Physical geography of India, Karnataka districts, rivers, soils, climate, natural resources
- **Polity (ರಾಜ್ಯಶಾಸ್ತ್ರ)**: Indian Constitution, Panchayati Raj (critical for VAO), Fundamental Rights, DPSP, State Government structure, 73rd/74th Amendments
- **Economy (ಆರ್ಥಿಕತೆ)**: Basic economics, agriculture, rural development, Karnataka economy, government schemes
- **General Science (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ವಿಜ್ಞಾನ)**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology basics, environmental science

#### General Kannada Sub-topics (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಕನ್ನಡ)

- Grammar (ವ್ಯಾಕರಣ): Sandhi, Samasa, Vibhakti, Pratyaya (ಪ್ರತ್ಯಯ)
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, antonyms, idioms (ಪರ್ಯಾಯ/ವಿರೋಧಿ ಪದಗಳು, ಗಾದೆಗಳು)
- Comprehension (ಅರ್ಥಮಾಡಿಕೆ)
- Translation basics (ಅನುವಾದ)

#### General English Sub-topics (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಇಂಗ್ಲೀಷ್) — see [[03_Notes/09_General_English]]

- Grammar: Tenses, verb patterns, prepositions, error spotting, subject-verb agreement
- Vocabulary: Synonyms, antonyms, one-word substitution, idioms/phrasal verbs
- Comprehension (passage-based questions)
- Letter writing (formal/informal)

#### Computer Knowledge Sub-topics (ಕಂಪ್ಯೂಟರ್ ಜ್ಞಾನ) — see [[03_Notes/08_Computer_Knowledge]]

- Basic computer fundamentals (hardware, software, OS)
- Internet & email, social media basics
- Current affairs (tech terms, recent digital initiatives in Karnataka)
- Abbreviations (भારतीय/ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ — e.g. CPU, RAM, USB, PAN, Aadhaar, e-SANAD)
