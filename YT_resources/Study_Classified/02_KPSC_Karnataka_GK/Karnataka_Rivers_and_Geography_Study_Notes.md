# 🌊 Karnataka Rivers & Physical Geography — Comprehensive Study Guide

> **Source Lectures**: Classic Education (Manjunath C), Rudresh Reddy Geography Marathon, and Inspire Bharat Tribals Short.

## 1. Cauvery (ಕಾವೇರಿ) River Basin System
🎥 **Lecture**: [Cauvery River Tributaries & Dams — Manjunath C, Classic Education (33m)](https://youtu.be/0oPY66rWSGo) `(Kannada)`

- **Origin**: Talakaveri in the Brahmagiri Hills (Kodagu district) at an elevation of 1,341 m.
- **Total Length**: ~805 km (~320 km within Karnataka).
- **Flow Direction**: Eastward towards Bay of Bengal (Tamil Nadu).
- **Key Tributaries in Karnataka**:
  - **Left Bank Tributaries**: Harangi (Somwarpet), Hemavati (Mudigere), Shimsha (Channarayapatna), Arkavathi (Nandi Hills).
  - **Right Bank Tributaries**: Lakshmanatirtha, Kabini (Kapila, origins in Wayanad), Suvarnavathi (Gajanoor).
- **Prominent Dams & Reservoirs**:
  - **KRS (Krishna Raja Sagara)**: Mandya district (built under Sir M. Visvesvaraya, Diwan of Mysore).
  - **Gorur Dam**: Across Hemavati river (Hassan district).
  - **Kabini Dam**: Heggadadevanakote (Mysuru district).
- **Famous Waterfalls**: Chunchanakatte Falls, Shivanasamudra Falls (Gaganachukki and Bharachukki — site of Asia's first hydroelectric power station, 1902), Hogenakkal Falls (border).

## 2. Krishna (ಕೃಷ್ಣ) River Basin System
🎥 **Lecture**: [Krishna River System in Karnataka (15m)](https://youtu.be/RM3cgHwQ3jI) `(Kannada)` | [Complete Karnataka Geography Marathon — Rudresh Reddy (2h 19m)](https://youtu.be/P12Ygye5zyE)

- **Origin**: Mahabaleshwar in the Western Ghats (Maharashtra).
- **Flow in Karnataka**: Enters Karnataka at Jamkhandi (Bagalkote district).
- **Key Tributaries in Karnataka**:
  - **Ghataprabha**: Originates in Maharashtra, famous Gokak Falls (resembles miniature Niagara). Hydroelectric project.
  - **Malaprabha**: Originates at Kanakumbi (Belagavi), historic sites of Aihole, Pattadakal, Badami on its banks. Navilatirtha Dam (Renuka Sagara).
  - **Bhima**: Major left-bank tributary originating in Bhimashankar (Maharashtra), joins Krishna near Raichur border.
  - **Tungabhadra (Pampa)**: Confluence of Tunga and Bhadra at Koodli (Shivamogga). Historic capital of Vijayanagara Empire (Hampi). TB Dam at Hosapete (Vijayanagara district).
- **Prominent Dams in Karnataka**:
  - **Almatti Dam (Lal Bahadur Shastri Reservoir)**: Upper Krishna Project (Bagalkote/Vijayapura).
  - **Narayanpur Dam (Basava Sagara)**: Yadgir district.
  - **Bhadra Dam (Lakkavalli)**: Chikkamagaluru district.

## 3. Indigenous & Tribal Communities (ಕಾಡು ಜನ / ಬುಡಕಟ್ಟು ಜನಾಂಗಗಳು)

| Tribal Community | Primary Geographic Region | Key Cultural Features & Livelihood | Special Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jenu Kuruba** | Kodagu, Mysuru, Chamarajanagar (Nagarahole, Bandipur) | Traditional honey-gatherers, shifting cultivation, forest lore | PVTG (Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group) |
| **Koraga** | Udupi, Dakshina Kannada, Uttara Kannada | Traditional basket weavers, bamboo artisans, unique language | PVTG (Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Group) |
| **Soliga** | Biligirirangana Hills (BR Hills, Chamarajanagar) | Sholaga dialect, conservation of forest bio-diversity, Ragi cuisine | Forest Rights Act title holders |
| **Kadu Kuruba / Betta Kuruba** | Mysuru, Kodagu | Bamboo craftsmanship, elephant handlers (Mahouts) | Scheduled Tribe |
| **Siddi** | Uttara Kannada (Yellapur, Haliyal, Ankola) | Descendants of Bantu peoples from Southeast Africa; unique music (Damami) | Scheduled Tribe |
