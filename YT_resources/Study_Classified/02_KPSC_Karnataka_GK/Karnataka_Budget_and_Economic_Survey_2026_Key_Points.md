# 📊 Karnataka Budget 2026-27 & Economic Survey 2025-26 Key Highlights

> **Source Lectures**: Saritha Madam (Current Affairs) & Puneeth Forum Economic Survey Analysis.

## 1. Macroeconomic Snapshot of Karnataka
🎥 **Economic Survey Lectures**: [Karnataka Economic Survey 2025-26 Summary — Puneeth Forum (28m)](https://youtu.be/9QL2s05JtpQ) `(Kannada)` | [Economic Survey Part 1 (22m)](https://youtu.be/cg6eZ1z_OJE) `(Kannada)`

- **GSDP Growth**: Karnataka continues to be among India's fastest-growing major states, propelled by the IT/ITeS services hub in Bengaluru, aerospace & defense manufacturing, and biotechnology.
- **Sectoral Contribution to GSVA**:
  - **Services**: ~64% (dominant driver of state economic output).
  - **Industry / Manufacturing**: ~21%.
  - **Agriculture & Allied**: ~15% (employs over 40% of the rural workforce).

## 2. Fiscal Metrics & Budgetary Architecture (2026-27)
🎥 **Budget Lecture**: [Karnataka Budget 2026-27 Detailed Breakdown — Saritha Madam (1h 17m)](https://youtu.be/nOFlMCpeX-g) `(Kannada)`

- **Total Budget Outlay**: Surpassing ₹3.8 Lakh Crore.
- **Fiscal Deficit**: Maintained within the FRBM Act permissible ceiling (<3% of GSDP).
- **Revenue Deficit / Surplus**: Balanced funding model accounting for social welfare commitments while sustaining capital expenditure in urban infrastructure, irrigation, and rural connectivity.

## 3. The Five Guarantee Schemes (ಪಂಚ ಗ್ಯಾರಂಟಿ ಯೋಜನೆಗಳು)

| Guarantee Scheme               | Target Beneficiary                   | Key Objective & Outlay Impact                                                           |
| :----------------------------- | :----------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Gruha Lakshmi (ಗೃಹಲಕ್ಷ್ಮಿ)** | Women head of households             | ₹2,000 monthly direct benefit transfer (DBT) for financial autonomy.                    |
| **Gruha Jyothi (ಗೃಹ ಜ್ಯೋತಿ)**  | Domestic households                  | Up to 200 units of free domestic electricity.                                           |
| **Shakti (ಶಕ್ತಿ)**             | Women across Karnataka               | Free bus travel in non-luxury KSRTC, BMTC, NWKRTC, KKRTC buses.                         |
| **Anna Bhagya (ಅನ್ನ ಭಾಗ್ಯ)**   | BPL cardholders                      | 10 kg foodgrains per person (including cash transfer component for 5 kg).               |
| **Yuva Nidhi (ಯುವ ನಿಧಿ)**      | Unemployed graduates/diploma holders | Monthly unemployment stipend (₹3,000 for degree, ₹1,500 for diploma) for up to 2 years. |

## 4. Key KPSC Exam Pointers

1. *NITI Aayog India Innovation Index*: Karnataka consistently ranks **#1 in the Major States category** due to high venture capital inflow, R&D institutes, and patent filings.
2. *Irrigation Priority*: Allocations for Upper Bhadra, Upper Krishna Project (Stage 3), and Yettinahole drinking water project.
3. *Direct Tax/GST Collection*: Karnataka stands 2nd nationwide in GST revenue mobilization after Maharashtra.
