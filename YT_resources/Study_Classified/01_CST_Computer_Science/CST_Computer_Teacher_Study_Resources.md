## 📑 Syllabus Mapping Overview

### 📘 Paper 2: Computer Science Core Units
- **Unit 1: Fundamentals of Computers**: Evolution, classification, hardware architecture, motherboards, memory devices. Key lecture: [[Unit01_Fundamentals_of_Computer_Study_Guide]].
- **Unit 3: Computer System Architecture & Digital Logic**: Logic gates (AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR, XOR), Boolean algebra, flip-flops, number systems. Key lecture: Dr. Gururaj Bulbule (Sadhana Academy).
- **Unit 4: Programming & Web Technologies**: C/C++, HTML/CSS, basic programming concepts.
- **Solved Papers & MCQs**: Previous GPSTR/PSTR/HSTR 2015-2022 computer papers, top 50/100 speed drills.

### 📙 Paper 1: General & Educational Pedagogy
- **Educational Psychology (ಶೈಕ್ಷಣಿಕ ಮನೋವಿಜ್ಞಾನ)**: Complete marathon by Shrishail Sir (Payana Academy). Crucial for Paper 1 high scoring.
- **General English (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಇಂಗ್ಲಿಷ್)**: Paper 1 grammar, comprehension, vocabulary by Suresh B N (Jnana Gangothri).
- **Computer Literacy (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯ ಕಂಪ್ಯೂಟರ್ ಸಾಕ್ಷರತೆ)**: Guaranteed 5-10 marks section.
